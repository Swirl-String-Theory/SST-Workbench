# A055 v0.4.1 preregistration

The protocol bundle is frozen in `preregistration/FROZEN_PROTOCOL.json`. The scientific formulas and thresholds are inherited unchanged from v0.4.0. The new version preregisters only execution/provenance hardening and gate-continuation semantics.

Official gate status remains fail-closed. `A055_DIAGNOSTIC_LEDGER.json` is explicitly non-authoritative and cannot convert a failed, unresolved, or prerequisite-blocked gate into PASS.
