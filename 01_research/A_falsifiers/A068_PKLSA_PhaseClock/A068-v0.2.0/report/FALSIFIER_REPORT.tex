\section{Research question and objective}
Can a source-native, materially bounded three-dimensional PKLSA vortex dynamically produce a reproducible intrinsic phase frequency, without using an imposed clock law, a target oscillation frequency, or a selected frozen-time eigenvalue? This version tests only whether the method and inherited diagnostics are controlled well enough to design a blind physical experiment.

No physical $H_0$ versus $H_1$ discrimination is possible with the present inputs.

\section{Scope and assumptions}
Three-dimensional, inviscid, unforced incompressible Euler flow in a periodic domain is the model when 3D velocity fields become available. A KnotPlot centreline, a Gaussian-smoothed starting field, or a two-wavepacket detector curve is not itself a measured material-vortex phase clock. The supplied A064 data represent one geometry-only provenance group. The two covariance-gap signals are correlated, not independent physical oscillators. No SI conversion is allowed without a calibrated source length and time scale.

% SST-REPORT-SECTION:EQUATIONS
\section{Mathematical model and observables}
The upstream governing equations are the incompressible Euler system \cite{Saffman1992},
\begin{equation}\partial_t\mathbf u+(\mathbf u\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u=-\frac{\nabla p}{\rhoF},\qquad \nabla\cdot\mathbf u=0.\tag{E1}\end{equation}
Vorticity is $\bm\omega=\nabla\times\mathbf u$. From squared-vorticity weights on a registered three-dimensional material region $M(t)$ one may construct the covariance $C_{ij}$. With ordered eigenvalues $\lambda_1\geq\lambda_2\geq\lambda_3$:
\begin{equation}
q_1=\frac{\lambda_1-\lambda_2}{\operatorname{tr}C},\qquad q_2=\frac{\lambda_2-\lambda_3}{\operatorname{tr}C}.\tag{E2}
\end{equation}
These variables are rotation invariant under a consistently transformed domain and region; they do not prove material-tube transport or a topological state. After centering, define a numerical analytic signal \cite{Gabor1946}
\begin{equation}
\phi_j(t)=\operatorname{unwrap}\arg\big[(q_j-\overline q_j)+i\,\mathcal{H}(q_j-\overline q_j)\big].\tag{E3}
\end{equation}
The measured slope (only on predeclared fitting intervals) is
\begin{equation}
(\widehat a_j,\widehat\Omega_j)=\operatorname*{arg\,min}_{a,b}\sum_{k\in W}\big(\phi_j(t_k)-a-bt_k\big)^2,\qquad \widehat f_j=\widehat\Omega_j/(2\pi).\tag{E4}
\end{equation}
In (E4) the notation means the $b$ component of the least-squares minimizer. Without SI-calibrated time, $\widehat f$ is only cycles per numerical time unit. The algorithm is a diagnostic, not a physical oscillator Hamiltonian.

\section{Numerical and statistical qualification}
The current synthetic positive control has input $f=0.25$ cycles per artificial time unit and a 16-cycle record. Proposed development regression requires frequency absolute error below $10^{-5}$ for both signals, at least 6 observed cycles, phase-fit RMS below $0.20\,\mathrm{rad}$, frequency coefficient of variation below $0.12$, and relative cross-signal frequency mismatch below $0.03$. These are \emph{method thresholds}, not physical SST acceptance criteria. For the later physical experiment, $N,2N,4N$ meshes, time-step halving, fixed final time and independent core refinement must be separately preregistered.

A later discovery/holdout study must choose independent source units, sample sizes, fixed windows and multiplicity corrections before examining outcomes.

% SST-REPORT-SECTION:INTERPRETATION
\section{Interpretation and falsification limits}
A synthetic control PASS means the diagnostic is working on that control; it does not validate vortex clocks. A064's instantaneous eigenpairs are not a time-harmonic Kelvin basis, and no observed velocity field has established a material knotted vortex oscillator. Failures of one candidate mode or packet launch cannot falsify every vortex-frequency mechanism. A consistent time-dependent, materially supported and converged field would permit the next physical campaign, not retroactive promotion of these data.
