# A056-v0.3.0 preregistration

## Question
For qualified E010/PKLSA trefoil carriers under a frozen finite-core/Euler perturbation experiment, do the generated residual Kelvin phase field and projected mode envelope require the Sine-Gordon and/or Mittag-Leffler candidates beyond the registered alternatives?

## Provider preregistration
The carrier selection, perturbation mode/amplitude, phase observable, envelope, solver, time window and resolution ladder are fixed in `configs/provider_*.json` before A056 scoring. The private carrier/source reveal is committed before model fitting.

## Independence
Resolution replays of one carrier are never independent evidence. E010 source/provider groups can demonstrate simulation replication, but `evidence_class=simulation` remains ineligible for physical G7 under framework v1.0.4.

## Falsification
A candidate is rejected for a provider family if it fails discovery, held-out confirmation, C++ certification, provider numerical validity, or registered same-carrier resolution convergence. Ordinary exponential/bi-exponential relaxation, KG/Duffing/wave phase laws, or no stable candidate are valid negative outcomes.
