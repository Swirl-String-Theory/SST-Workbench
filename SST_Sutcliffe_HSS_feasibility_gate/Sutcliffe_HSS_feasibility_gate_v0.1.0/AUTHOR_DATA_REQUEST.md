# Draft data request to the HSS authors

Subject: Numerical centreline and framing data for “Hopf solitons and elastic rods”

Dear Prof. Harland, Prof. Speight, and Prof. Sutcliffe,

I am reproducing the elastic-rod calculations in your paper “Hopf solitons and
elastic rods,” Phys. Rev. D 83, 065008 (2011), as a preregistered study of a
renormalized non-local extension.

Could you let me know whether any of the following 2010/2011 numerical material
still exists and may be shared for research reproducibility?

- final polygon vertices for the buckled, linked, and trefoil rod minima;
- material-frame or Bishop-frame angle data;
- simulated-annealing source code and parameter files;
- Skyrme–Faddeev field arrays or extracted preimage curves used in the
  comparison figures;
- conventions for component orientation and twist allocation.

The project is independent of particle-physics calibration and will cite the
original paper as the source of the model and benchmark. If the data are no
longer available, confirmation of that fact would also be useful.

Kind regards,

Omar Iskandarani
