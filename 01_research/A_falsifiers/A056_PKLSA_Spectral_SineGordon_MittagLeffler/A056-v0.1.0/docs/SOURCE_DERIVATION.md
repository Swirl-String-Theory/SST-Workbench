# Source-image derivation note

A056 was motivated by two user-supplied mathematical reference images.

- Image 1 contains the spectral theorem formula and the Sine–Gordon equation among several unrelated equations. A056 uses the spectral theorem only to justify spectral/POD decomposition of a self-adjoint covariance operator, and tests Sine–Gordon as a physical reduced closure.
- Image 2 combines two distinct Mittag-Leffler topics: the meromorphic-function Mittag-Leffler theorem and a graph of generalized Mittag-Leffler functions. A056 uses the **generalized Mittag-Leffler function**, not the meromorphic principal-part theorem, for the fractional-memory ringdown lane.

This distinction is frozen to avoid conflating same-name mathematical results.
