This is a \textbf{numerical model} initialized by PKLSA/KnotPlot geometry; it is \emph{not} a confirmed source-native material vortex or an independent physical PKLSA clock. Previously inspected geometry and past diagnostics remain \textbf{unblinded development evidence}; official BLIND packaging does not retroactively restore epistemic blindness.

\section{Actual continuous and numerical model}
The finite-dimensional Galerkin operator implements the projected Lamb form of Euler:
\begin{equation}\partial_t\mathbf u=\mathcal P[\mathbf u\times\bm\omega],\quad \bm\omega=\nabla\times\mathbf u,\quad \nabla\cdot\mathbf u=0.\label{eq:euler}\end{equation}
The original A064 code deposits a signed line-current along each polygon edge (cloud-in-cell), applies Gaussian finite-core smoothing and a two-thirds wavenumber cutoff, then constructs the divergence-free Fourier Biot--Savart velocity
\begin{equation}\widehat{\mathbf u}_0(\mathbf k)=\frac{i\mathbf k\times\widehat{\bm\omega}_a(\mathbf k)}{|\mathbf k|^2}\quad (\mathbf k\ne0).\label{eq:biotsavart}\end{equation}
RK4 evolves this field without inserted phase. Conservation screens use
\begin{equation}
E=\frac{1}{2V}\int_V|\mathbf u|^2\,dV,\quad H=\frac1V\int_V\mathbf u\cdot\bm\omega\,dV,\quad Z=\frac{1}{2V}\int_V|\bm\omega|^2\,dV.\label{eq:ehz}
\end{equation}
For the full periodic volume, use enstrophy weights to construct the periodic-centered three-dimensional covariance tensor $C$. Ordered eigenvalues define the shape scalar
\begin{equation}q_{\rm sh}=\frac{\lambda_1-\lambda_2}{\operatorname{tr}C},\quad q_{\rm sh,2}=\frac{\lambda_2-\lambda_3}{\operatorname{tr}C}.\end{equation}
These two eigenvalue gaps are correlated. Instead of treating them as independent physical clocks, use the distinct flow functional
\begin{equation}q_Z=\frac{L^2 Z}{E}.\label{eq:qz}\end{equation}
Both $q_{\rm sh}$ and $q_Z$ are computed from each actual evolved 3D field, not from imposed oscillators. The analytic-signal phase estimator is
\begin{equation}
\phi_q(t)=\operatorname{unwrap}\arg\{[q(t)-\bar q]+i\mathcal H[q(t)-\bar q]\},\qquad \widehat\Omega_q=\operatorname{slope}_{W}\phi_q(t).\label{eq:phase}
\end{equation}
This estimator is a screen only; it does not establish a relative periodic orbit or intrinsic clock. Thresholds were specified in method preflight: at least six unwrapped cycles; RMS phase-fit error at most 0.20 radians; instantaneous frequency CV at most 0.12; cross-frequency relative error at most 0.03. A constant shape under rigid rotation is no clock. A few cycles of a windowed transient are not a proof of stability. Time series are uniformly sampled by construction.

\subsection*{v0.4.4 material and stability operators}
The finite-core tube seeded around the native trefoil is a \emph{candidate} material
surface.  At every Euler RK4 substage its nodes obey
\begin{equation}
\frac{d\mathbf X}{dt}=\mathbf u(\mathbf X,t),\quad
\eta_{\omega}=\frac1A\int_S\frac{|\bm\omega\cdot\mathbf n|}{\max(|\bm\omega|,\epsilon_{\omega})}\,dA,\quad
\delta_V=\frac{|V(t)-V(0)|}{|V(0)|}.
\end{equation}
The prescribed screening limits are $\delta_V<0.05$, area-mean
$\eta_{\omega}<0.05$, and pointwise maximum $|\widehat\omega\cdot\mathbf n|<0.10$,
at all retained snapshots.  Net vorticity flux through a closed surface is
\emph{not} an admissible vortex-tube test; it vanishes even on surfaces not
made of vortex lines.  Initial tube parameters are fixed at 32 axial
sections, 10 azimuthal sides and radius 0.12 model units.

The full-field symmetry-reduced return statistic is
\begin{equation}
 r_{\mathrm{RPO}}(T,g)=\frac{\|\mathbf u(T)-g\mathbf u(0)\|_{L^2(V)}}
 {\|\mathbf u(0)\|_{L^2(V)}},\qquad
 g\in\mathbb T^3\rtimes O^{+}_{\mathrm{cube}}.
\end{equation}
The implementation registers a $0.025$ relative candidate screen and searches
all 24 proper cubic rotations with 3D periodic translations.  This is a
\emph{full 3D-field search}, not proof of a converged nonlinear RPO.
Repeated returns, neutral drift modes, shooting and mesh/time convergence
remain necessary.

The independent tangent equation is evaluated using the unmodified A064
Euler linearization,
\begin{equation}
\dot{\delta\mathbf u}=\mathcal P[\delta\mathbf u\times\bm\omega+
\mathbf u\times(\nabla\times\delta\mathbf u)],\qquad
M_g=g^{-1}D\Phi^{T_R}(\mathbf u_0),
\end{equation}
where $M_gv=\mu v$, $\Omega_F=|\arg\mu|/T_R$ and
$\epsilon_F=\|M_gv-\mu v\|/\|M_gv\|$.  This is an \emph{operator}
frequency; a measured $q$-phase never enters the tangent propagator.
Ritz values from a five-vector fixed-seed subspace are diagnostics only.
No physical Floquet frequency can be reported without a validated RPO,
residual, spectral convergence and a withheld prediction comparison.
