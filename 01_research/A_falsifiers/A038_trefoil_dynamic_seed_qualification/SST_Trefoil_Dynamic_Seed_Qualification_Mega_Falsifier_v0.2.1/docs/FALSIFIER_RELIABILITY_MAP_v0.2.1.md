## Evidence rules for future registry promotion

A registry physics status may be strengthened only when the evidence bundle contains a frozen hypothesis and thresholds, code/config/dataset hashes, an independent or held-out dataset, blind identity commitment where applicable, convergence and coverage results, raw machine-readable outputs, and a reveal record. Post-hoc analyses remain separately labeled and cannot overwrite the preregistered verdict.
