# A054-v0.3.0 preregistration — broad low-crossing topology discovery

The purpose is candidate discovery, not confirmation of the historical 5_2/6_1 assignment.

## Candidate knot set
All prime knots through seven crossings: 3_1, 4_1, 5_1, 5_2, 6_1, 6_2, 6_3, 7_1 ... 7_7.

## Source skeletons
Four source-native KnotPlot embeddings: 6^3_1, 6^3_2 (Borromean), 6^3_3, and an independently exported T(3,3) embedding. 6^3_3 and T(3,3) intentionally remain separate geometry-provider/embedding replicates although topologically equivalent.

## Direct source-native links
6^3_1, 6^3_2, 6^3_3, T(3,3), T(6,9), T(6,15), T(6,21).

## Balanced BASIC design
- 14 knots × 4 linked source skeletons as homogeneous K,K,K decorations = 56
- 14 unlinked K,K,K controls = 14
- 6 historical mixed 5_2/6_1 permutations × 4 linked skeletons = 24
- 7 direct source-native links = 7
- total = 101 anonymous cases

The eight circulation sectors and all numerical thresholds are inherited unchanged from the pre-reveal v0.1.1 BASIC protocol. No mass, charge, Standard Model identity, desired knot, or desired ranking is encoded.

No weighted winner score is defined. Candidates are compared by qualification, polarity behavior, relative-equilibrium residual, cross-stabilization and far-field anisotropy. Any promising candidate must later pass the v0.2.0 restoring/Kelvin/ringdown and conditional RPO/Floquet certification.

## Sequential execution rule (frozen before Stage A)
Stage A evaluates all 101 candidates with the same two spatial resolutions, finite-core static observables, objectivity test and eight polarity sectors, but no time integration. A candidate is eligible for Stage B only when it is numerically qualified and passes the preregistered qualitative 2+1 polarity gate in both circulation normalizations. Stage B then restores short-horizon dynamics; v0.2.0 restoring/Kelvin/ringdown/RPO-Floquet certification remains the final dynamical layer. No top-N cap or semantic identity enters the Stage-A selection.
