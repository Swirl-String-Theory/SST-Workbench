# v0.4.1 hotfix — portable PKLSA manifest

The v0.4.0 Windows run could pass native build, all 18 tests and the strict blind guard, then stop during PKLSA verification because the embedded v0.1.0 atlas manifest contained two CPython-3.13 bytecode cache records. Bytecode caches are interpreter/runtime products and must not be signed dataset artifacts.

v0.4.1 embeds PKLSA v0.1.1 with the exact same 2352 geometry candidates and candidate IDs, but a portable manifest excluding `__pycache__` and `*.pyc`. The verifier also ignores such records defensively.

Scientific protocol: unchanged from v0.4.0.
