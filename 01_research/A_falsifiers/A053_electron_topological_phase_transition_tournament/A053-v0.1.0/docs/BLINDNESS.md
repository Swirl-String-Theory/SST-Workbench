# Blindness protocol

The public package and default configs contain no canonical SST constants and no electron energy target.

Blind output hashes are frozen before reveal. Reveal may attach SI interpretations to existing values but may not alter topology labels, gate states, thresholds, case inclusion, or tournament survivors.

Forbidden in blind producer/evaluator configuration:

- `alpha`;
- electron rest energy or mass;
- canonical SST swirl speed or core radius;
- requested final topology;
- transition-frequency targets;
- parameters optimized against reveal targets.
