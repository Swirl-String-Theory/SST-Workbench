\section{Geometry and circulation}
For oriented disjoint closed curves $C_i,C_j\subset\mathbb R^3$,
\begin{equation}\mathrm{Lk}(C_i,C_j)=\frac{1}{4\pi}
\oint_{C_i}\oint_{C_j}\frac{(d\mathbf r_i\times d\mathbf r_j)
\cdot(\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j)}{\lVert\mathbf r_i-\mathbf r_j\rVert^3}.
\end{equation}
The Biot--Savart velocity generated by a filament $C_j$ at circulation
$\Gamma\ [\mathrm{m^2\,s^{-1}}]$ obeys
\begin{equation}\mathbf u_j(\mathbf x)=\frac{\Gamma}{4\pi}
\oint_{C_j}\frac{d\mathbf r_j\times(\mathbf x-\mathbf r_j)}
{\lVert\mathbf x-\mathbf r_j\rVert^3},\quad
\oint_{C_i}\mathbf u_j\cdot d\boldsymbol\ell=\Gamma\,\mathrm{Lk}(C_i,C_j).
\end{equation}
The normalized circulation is dimensionless. When KnotPlot coordinates are
unitless, no measured SI conversion may be inferred. A finite regulator $a$
replaces $r^{-3}$ by $(r^2+a^2)^{-3/2}$; this regulates the field but does not
preserve exact topological quantization at finite $a$.
