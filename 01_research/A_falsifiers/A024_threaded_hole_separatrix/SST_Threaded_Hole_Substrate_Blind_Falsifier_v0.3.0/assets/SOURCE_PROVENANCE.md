# Source provenance

Primary twist carriers embedded in this package:

- `4_1` -> `assets/fseries/knot.4_1.fseries`
- `5_2` -> `assets/fseries/knot.5_2.fseries`
- `6_1` -> `assets/fseries/knot.6_1.fseries`
- `7_2` -> `assets/fseries/knot.7_2.fseries`

They originate from the Fremlin Fourier-series dataset used in the SST/VortexLab workbench. Source comments containing `must be checked`, `converted to 6-column`, `Data source: Brian Gilbert`, or `Fourier projection` are excluded from primary use.

User-created ideal-to-Fourier conversions such as the later `9_1`/`10_1` additions are intentionally **not** bundled as independent Fremlin evidence. They may later be admitted only as a separately labelled `DERIVED_CONTROL` stratum.

v0.2.0 additionally applies exact geometric qualification before blinding. Source provenance PASS does not guarantee geometric PASS: a source-clean carrier/thread construction can still be excluded for insufficient finite-core clearance or inadequate thread linking.
