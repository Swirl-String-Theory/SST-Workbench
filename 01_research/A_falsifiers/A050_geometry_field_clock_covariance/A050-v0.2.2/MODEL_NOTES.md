# MODEL NOTES — v0.2.2

This release separates discovery from confirmation. Base G-family trajectories remain useful for spatial and temporal-memory continuity, but the primary modal-persistence verdict is carried by pre-generated blind holdouts whose geometry identities are not available to the executable blind path.

The pressure-like scalar remains the dimensionless periodic elliptic closure used in v0.2.x. Temporal-memory convergence is a property of the generated reference dynamics and closure under this protocol; it is not an absolute clock prediction.

The modal gate measures coherent transverse deformation of a closed filament after rigid alignment and tangential-gauge removal. Passing establishes a persistent numerical transverse branch under the stated discretization tests. It does not by itself establish a physical wave speed or identify a unique microscopic mechanism.

No Floquet multiplier is estimated in v0.2.2. The prior long return search did not find a qualified recurrence; searching longer until a return appears would introduce a stopping-rule problem. A future RPO campaign must be preregistered separately.
