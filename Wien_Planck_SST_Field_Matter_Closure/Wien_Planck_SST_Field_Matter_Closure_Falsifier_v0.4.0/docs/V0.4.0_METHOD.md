# v0.4.0 method change

v0.4.0 is a major-population release, not a threshold tweak.

## Population change

v0.3.x tested the 48-member PTSA trefoil family. v0.4.0 embeds PKLSA v0.1.0 and begins from 49 historical topology-family labels × 48 constructive shader-inspired variants = 2352 candidates.

## Preserved v0.3.1 corrections

The following remain unchanged in principle:

- iterative, multi-cycle frequency certification;
- rejection of first-FFT-bin/window-limited pseudo-frequencies;
- gauge-normal relative-equilibrium residual;
- adaptive arclength reparameterization with pre-cleanup mesh diagnostics;
- target-free broadband discovery followed by a frozen normal mode;
- energy and frequency measured on that same frozen mode;
- explicit campaign coverage gate;
- temporal and spatial convergence prerequisites;
- classical \(J\propto A^2\) null and amplitude-independence test;
- strict separation of dimensionless discovery from post-reveal SI normalization.

## New preflight gates

The scientific campaign refuses to start unless the public funnel proves the expected atlas/funnel counts. When the screening backend is SYCL, a GPU↔CPU parity artifact with `pass=true` is also mandatory. The blind analyzer records this as `UA0c_PKLSA_funnel_preflight`.

A CPU-fallback funnel is allowed, but is explicitly labelled and does not require a GPU parity artifact.
