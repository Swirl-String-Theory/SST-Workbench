```tex
G = G_0\left(\frac{\rho_f}{\rho_{\rm core}}\right)^k\left(\frac{\vchar}{c}\right)^n\pi^p2^m,
\qquad G_0=\frac{\vchar^2r_c}{M_e}.
```

Ranges:

```text
k in [-3, 3]
n in [-20, 20]
p in [-8, 8]
m in [-12, 12]
```

This gives 121,975 candidates.  The v3 audit finds 31 candidates within 5% of `G_N` and 5 candidates within 0.575%.  A better numerical hit exists than the SST-seed candidate.  Therefore the sub-percent match has no evidentiary force without a prior derivation of the exponent structure and kernel.

Rule going forward: **freeze, derive, then compare**.  No additional coefficient searches count as support.
