% SST-REPORT-SECTION:QUESTION
\section{Research question and objective}
Can a smooth incompressible inviscid vortex model simultaneously satisfy: (i) material preservation of the zero/nonzero vorticity population; (ii) material-volume and vorticity-flux conservation; (iii) a locally irrotational but nontrivial exterior velocity field with circulation periods and nonlocal kinetic energy; and (iv) the original static relaxed-centerline relative-equilibrium test?

The objective is not to prove an SST interpretation. It is to make the Helmholtz-to-SST partition testable with explicit observables, provenance, convergence, and backend certification. The static centerline branch cannot establish actual long-time Euler persistence or reconnection avoidance.

% SST-REPORT-SECTION:HYPOTHESES
\section{Competing hypotheses and allowed conclusions}
\paragraph{Null hypothesis $H_0$.}
At least one preregistered consequence fails beyond tolerance: the exact material control does not preserve the vorticity class/volume/flux, the reconstructed exterior is not locally potential, exterior kinetic energy is unresolved, the far-field exponent is unstable on eligible closed sources, or an input fails one of the unchanged v0.1.1 centerline gates.

\paragraph{Alternative hypothesis $H_1$.}
The exact material controls close numerically; the exterior is locally divergence-free/curl-free but can retain nonzero speed and circulation periods; a resolved energy fraction lies outside the near-core source zone; eligible closed sources have stable far-field scaling; and each source geometry receives an independent H0--H4 classification.

\paragraph{Falsification semantics.}
Each H* or P* branch reports independently after G0/G1/G2. Aggregate FAIL means at least one eligible input failed that branch; per-input results remain in \texttt{BLIND\_SAMPLE\_METRICS.json}. Backend failure is certification evidence only. No threshold may be moved after freeze.

% SST-REPORT-SECTION:ASSUMPTIONS
\section{Assumptions, domain of validity, and known edge cases}
\begin{enumerate}[label=A\arabic*.]
\item Smooth three-dimensional incompressible Euler flow, no viscosity, no external nonconservative forcing.
\item Source geometries are closed oriented filaments. Finite-core regularization is used only where self-interaction/energy would otherwise be singular.
\item The blind geometry branch uses source units and unit circulation. No physical density or absolute length calibration participates in blind scoring.
\item P3 samples only exterior points. P5 uses a geometric near-core mask and therefore tests nonlocal velocity-energy support, not exact compact vorticity support.
\item P6 excludes near-zero-vector-area geometries because cancellation may remove the leading localized-source far-field moment.
\end{enumerate}
The experiment does not cover viscosity, compressibility, reconnection, singular Euler breakdown, or externally forced vorticity production.

\section{Exact mathematical model and observables}
The central continuum relations are
\begin{align}
\vort &= \nabla\times\vel, \\
\vort(\mathbf{X}(\mathbf{a},t),t) &= \Fmat(\mathbf{a},t)\vort_0(\mathbf{a}), \\
J&=\det\Fmat=1, \\
\Phi_\omega&=\int_{S(t)}\vort\cdot d\mathbf{S}=\Gamma=\mathrm{const}. 
\end{align}
For the numerical filament reconstruction,
\begin{equation}
\vel(\mathbf{x})=\frac{\Gamma}{4\pi}\oint_C
\frac{d\boldsymbol{\ell}'\times(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}')}{(|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'|^2+a^2)^{3/2}},
\end{equation}
with $a=0$ only for exterior evaluations that remain separated from the filament. The energy-length coefficient is
\begin{equation}
E=\rho\Gamma^2\ell_E,\qquad
\ell_E=\frac{1}{8\pi}\oint\!\oint
\frac{d\boldsymbol{\ell}\cdot d\boldsymbol{\ell}'}{\sqrt{|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'|^2+a^2}}.
\end{equation}
Relative equilibrium is tested after fitting rigid translation, rigid rotation, and tangential reparameterization. Exterior topology is tested through
\begin{equation}
\Gamma^{-1}\oint_\gamma\vel\cdot d\boldsymbol{\ell}\approx \operatorname{Lk}(\gamma,C).
\end{equation}
The exterior differential audit tests $\nabla\cdot\vel=0$ and $\nabla\times\vel=0$. The energy partition observable is
\begin{equation}
f_{\mathrm{ext}}=
\frac{\int_{V\setminus V_{\mathrm{core}}}\frac12|\vel|^2\,dV}
{\int_V\frac12|\vel|^2\,dV}.
\end{equation}
Far-field shells fit
\begin{equation}
\log u_{\mathrm{rms}}(r)=b-p\log r.
\end{equation}
A steady irrotational Bernoulli interpretation maps the frozen velocity exponent to $|\Delta p|\propto r^{-2p}$ and $|\nabla p|\propto r^{-(2p+1)}$; this is a post-fit interpretation, not a fitting constraint.

\section{Interpretation, limits, falsifiable predictions, and minimal follow-up experiments}
P0--P2 test the exact material-population logic of the smooth incompressible model, not a full time-integrated knot evolution. H3 remains the original strong static-centerline shape test and can fail while the matter--field partition branch remains informative. P3/P4 establish whether the computed exterior is locally potential yet globally circulation-bearing. P5 tests whether velocity-energy support extends beyond the near-core source zone. P6 supplies a frozen far-field exponent for later mechanism comparison.

A minimal next experiment after v0.2.0 is a genuinely time-dependent Euler/finite-core evolution in which material tracers are initialized both inside and outside a vortex tube, then tested for support-class preservation, circulation conservation, and topology change under simultaneous spatial and temporal refinement. A second independent experiment should reconstruct pressure from the time-dependent velocity field rather than infer pressure solely from a steady exterior relation.
