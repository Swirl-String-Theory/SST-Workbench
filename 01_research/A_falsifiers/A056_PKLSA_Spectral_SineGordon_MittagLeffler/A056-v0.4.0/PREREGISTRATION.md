# A056-v0.4.0 preregistration

## Primary question

Does the phase-topology-aware circular/Fourier G2 qualifier admit a stable low-dimensional residual Kelvin field from fresh E010 dynamics, and, conditional on that admission, do Sine-Gordon and/or Mittag-Leffler candidates survive the unchanged blind discovery, holdout and numerical-certification chain?

## Development-set firewall

The observed A056-v0.3.0 filament `m=3` G2 diagnostics are a development/motivation set only. Their raw-POD overlaps are not used as v0.4.0 evidence and were not used to choose a permissive replacement threshold. Official real-provider evidence starts with the fresh preregistered `m=4`, `epsilon=0.03` perturbation.

## G2 representation and frozen thresholds

With \(z=e^{i\varphi}\) and \(\widetilde z=z-\langle z\rangle_s\), compute circular POD and discovery/confirmation normalized non-zero-mode Fourier-power distributions. A case passes spectral G2 iff all of:

\[
\eta_3^{\rm circ}\ge0.95,
\qquad
\epsilon_{\rm orth}^{\rm circ}\le10^{-10},
\qquad
\mathcal O_F=\sum_{n\ne0}\sqrt{P_n^DP_n^C}\ge0.90.
\]

Raw-phase early/late POD overlap is recorded against its historical 0.85 reference but is non-blocking. Circular-POD early/late subspace overlap is also diagnostic. The exact Mittag-Leffler identity at \(\alpha=1\) must satisfy the registered \(10^{-12}\) numerical tolerance.

The blocking representation is invariant to integer \(2\pi\) phase wrapping. Its circular-POD energy/orthogonality metrics are covariant under global phase multiplication and fixed cyclic spatial relabelling. The Fourier-power overlap is additionally insensitive to phase rotations of individual Fourier coefficients caused by cyclic translation. No claim is made that a time-dependent spatial reparameterization leaves every circular-POD statistic unchanged.

## Downstream model-selection policy

No SG/ML threshold is changed from v0.3.0. Phase discovery requires the registered positive-coefficient/condition/BIC criteria; memory discovery requires the registered BIC and interior-alpha criteria. Confirmation uses untouched holdout blocks. G5 requires deterministic coarsening/convergence plus C++/OpenMP FP64 parity. Resolution replays of one carrier never count as replication.

## Evidence semantics

E010 finite-core/Biot-Savart and Euler outputs are simulations. Different upstream-provider families may establish a replicated simulation conclusion, but physical G7 remains restricted to framework-eligible independent/experimental evidence.
