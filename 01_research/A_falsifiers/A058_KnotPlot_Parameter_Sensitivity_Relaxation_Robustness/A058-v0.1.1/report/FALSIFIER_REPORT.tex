\section{Research question}
Are historical KnotPlot-relaxed exports robust to relaxation, force, discretization and export settings, and do the historical scripts qualify as a canonical production geometry source?
\section{Interpretive boundary}
Relaxation is treated as a geometry-generation algorithm. A relaxed embedding is not evidence of intrinsic SST self-confinement or particle stability.
\section{Historical audit}
The supplied archive contains 50 scripts: 20 knot, 16 link and 14 torus constructions. The principal relaxation settings are constant across the archive; the scripts therefore do not constitute a parameter-sensitivity experiment. Their \code{save} commands do not explicitly pin \code{ascii/raw}, so output representation depends on KnotPlot's \code{sformat} state. The scripts also omit explicit settings for the documented relaxation commands \code{charge}, \code{hooke}, \code{power}, and \code{timeincr}; A058 records the runtime \code{version} and complete \code{parameters} state and treats those defaults as a provenance dependency until qualified.
\section{Observables}
For scalar metric $m$,
\begin{equation}\Delta_m=\frac{|m_c-m_0|}{\max(|m_0|,10^{-12})}.\end{equation}
For coordinate curves, a scale-free cyclic/reversal-aware Procrustes diagnostic is
\begin{equation}
D_{\rm shape}=\min_{R,\delta,\sigma}\left[\frac1M\sum_i\|\hat x_i-R\hat y_{\sigma i+\delta}\|^2\right]^{1/2}.
\end{equation}
Edge discretization is monitored through
\begin{equation}C_V(\ell)=\sigma(\ell_i)/\bar\ell.\end{equation}

\section{Campaign}
The preregistered pilot contains one same-topology dual-generator pair, one symmetry control, two additional single-component cases, and one multi-component link. It tests the full on/off cube of mechanical/electrical/bending forces, \code{max-dr}, \code{close}, \code{bencon}, \code{stusplit}, a bead-resolution ladder, and \code{dstep} as a null control. Checkpoints are 0, 250, 1000, 4000, 10000 and 15000 atomic relaxation iterations.
\section{Gate semantics}
All scientific gates G1--G8 depend only on G0, not on each other. Therefore an early FAIL never prevents later diagnostic gates from running. G8 may reject the historical protocol on provenance while G3--G7 still determine the appropriate replacement settings.

\section{Pre-registered thresholds}
The \code{dstep} null control must remain below $10^{-10}$ in scalar relative effect. Numerical-envelope median scalar effect must remain at or below $10^{-2}$. Baseline 10k--15k drift must remain at or below $10^{-2}$. Coordinate thresholds are $D_{\rm shape}\le0.005$ for plateau/parameter robustness and $\le0.01$ for the resolution comparison. Native FP64 parity tolerance is $10^{-12}$ relative.

\section{Allowed interpretation}
A G8 FAIL means the historical export protocol should not be promoted as canonical. It does not imply that its geometries are unusable. G3--G7 determine whether old geometries can remain comparison/legacy candidates and which parameter envelope should be frozen for a replacement canonical generation.
