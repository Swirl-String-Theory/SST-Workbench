\section{A029 v0.7 specific-Lagrangian curvature certification}

The v0.6 parent producer defines the linear material-parcel diagnostic
\begin{equation}
\delta \ell^{(1)}
=\mathbf v_0\!\cdot\!\delta\mathbf v-\frac{\delta p}{\rho_f},
\end{equation}
and the corresponding complex modal coefficient
\begin{equation}
C_\ell(r)=U(r)\hat u_z(r)+V(r)\hat u_\theta(r)-\hat\pi(r).
\end{equation}
The $O(\epsilon^2)$ kinetic term is not added because the linearized A029 state does not provide the matching second-order pressure/state correction.

For the closed-loop condition
\begin{equation}
\hat k\hat L+m\Theta_B=2\pi(n+\Delta),
\end{equation}
the symmetric residual is
\begin{equation}
K_\ell(\Delta,t)
=L_\ell(0,t)-\frac{L_\ell(-\Delta,t)+L_\ell(+\Delta,t)}{2}.
\end{equation}
Writing
\begin{equation}
\delta\hat k=\frac{2\pi\Delta}{\hat L},
\end{equation}
a smooth same-branch Taylor expansion gives
\begin{equation}
K_\ell
=-\frac{1}{2}\frac{\partial^2L_\ell}{\partial\hat k^2}
(\delta\hat k)^2+O((\delta\hat k)^4).
\end{equation}
Thus v0.7 defines
\begin{equation}
C_k(\delta\hat k,t)
\equiv -\frac{2K_\ell}{(\delta\hat k)^2}
=\frac{\partial^2L_\ell}{\partial\hat k^2}+O((\delta\hat k)^2).
\end{equation}

Using the two smallest detunings $d_1>d_2$,
\begin{equation}
C_k(0,t)\approx
\frac{d_1^2C_k(d_2,t)-d_2^2C_k(d_1,t)}{d_1^2-d_2^2}.
\end{equation}
This complex Richardson-extrapolated field is the primary local certification object.

The eigensolver retains a global complex phase freedom.  A physical real signal therefore requires an independently observed complex modal coefficient
\begin{equation}
a(t_0)=\epsilon e^{i\phi_0}
\end{equation}
projected on the exact same normalized basis.  If an independent physical model supplies $\delta\hat k_{\rm phys}$ inside the certified range,
\begin{equation}
K_{\rm phys}(t)\simeq-\frac12 C_k(0,t)(\delta\hat k_{\rm phys})^2,
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}
\delta\hat\ell_{\rm SST}(t)
=\epsilon\,\Re\!\left[e^{i\phi_0}K_{\rm phys}(t)\right].
\end{equation}
With independently sourced $a$ and $V_0$,
\begin{equation}
\delta\ell_{\rm SST}=V_0^2\delta\hat\ell_{\rm SST},\qquad
\delta s_{\rm SST}=aV_0\int\delta\hat\ell_{\rm SST}\,d\hat t.
\end{equation}
Finally, conditional on an independently inferred QGI scale,
\begin{equation}
\delta\phi_{\rm SST}=\frac{\delta s_{\rm SST}}{(\hbar/m)_{\rm QGI}}.
\end{equation}
