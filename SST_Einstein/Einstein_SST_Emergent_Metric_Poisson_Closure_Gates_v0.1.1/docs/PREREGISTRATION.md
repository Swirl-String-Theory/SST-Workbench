# Blind preregistration

Measurement occurs under salted IDs. Filenames are kept only in `blind_manifest_private.json` and are not present in `measurements_blind.csv`.

Primary gates are fixed before measurement:

1. **1/r monopole gate**: tail exponent of shell-mean \(v^2\) must be \(1\pm\epsilon\), and \(R\langle v^2\rangle/2\) must plateau.
2. **Pressure-Poisson monopole gate**: \(\mu_Q=I_Q/(4\pi)\) must plateau, be predominantly positive, and agree within the preregistered factor with \(\mu_{v^2}\).
3. **Pressure--Phi closure**: \(I_Q\) and \(I_\Phi\) must agree within the preregistered factor.

Secondary diagnostics: far-field anisotropy and Beltrami misalignment. They are reported separately.

The metric determinant and clock relation are algebraic checks of the chosen effective metric and are not counted as evidence.
