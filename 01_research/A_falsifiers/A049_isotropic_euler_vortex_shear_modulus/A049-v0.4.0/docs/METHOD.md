# Method — v0.4.1

1. Construct the approximately isotropic Hopf-linked finite-core vortex-tube ensemble and rasterize it into a periodic divergence-free vorticity field.
2. Reconstruct the velocity field spectrally and normalize the blind reference scale.
3. Fit the instantaneous Euler RHS to the translational group tangent of the field. Record the best drift velocity and normalized residual.
4. Evolve the background briefly and compare it with the exact Fourier-translated initial state. This is the relative-equilibrium prequalification.
5. Repeat the relative-equilibrium residual at a higher resolution to detect a purely coarse-grid artifact.
6. Run the original evolving-background tangent-linear helicity probes.
7. Run a longer frozen-background tangent diagnostic to distinguish background nonstationarity from the instantaneous linear operator response.
8. Seal blind outputs before reveal-only Master-Factor and proton/electron comparisons.
