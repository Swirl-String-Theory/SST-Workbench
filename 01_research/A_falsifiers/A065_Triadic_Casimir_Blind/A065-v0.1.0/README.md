## Three independently committed datasets

- `D01_EULER` — analytic incompressible stationary flows, strain/vorticity, pressure Poisson, local clock response;

Each dataset has its *own* SHA-256 tree seal in `seals/Dxx.seal.json`. `seals/GLOBAL_COMMITMENT.json` additionally binds all three independent seal roots and a **hash only** of the private comparator. Running BLIND reads only D01-D03 and verifies (but does not open) the comparator. REVEAL is impossible without all three intact seals and a verified blind-result hash. These are **tamper-evident commitments**, not digital signatures or protection from a dishonest party that rewrites the entire package. For strong independent provenance, publish the ZIP SHA-256 externally.

`private_reveal/targets.json` is included to remain fully self-contained, but isolated from BLIND execution. It contains **orthodox analytic toy comparators**, *not experimental Casimir measurements*.

## Physics scope and caveats

D01 controls are exact synthetic linear Euler fields, not time-dependent Euler solutions or real knotted-vortex data. D02 gauge-covariant atomic clock is an **SST hypothesis**, not an established matter-link identification. The 2x2 oscillator Hessian, bare delta-sheet stiffness, and finite-temperature Gaussian scalar reflection are *declared toy input assumptions*, never claimed as independently derived metal properties. D03 creates an attractive thermal scalar-sheet response if the given toy stiffnesses are positive. This is an implementation/identifiability check, not a zero-temperature Casimir derivation.

**No SST canonical constants, ħc, ideal-conductor amplitude, or Casimir force are fitted in BLIND.** The symbolic model has unspecified microscopic normalization; no physical amplitude prediction is made. Re-running with different toy couplings does not amount to independent physical validation.
