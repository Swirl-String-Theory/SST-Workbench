# Equations

For a closed centerline of measured length \(L_N\) at resolution \(N\), E012
assigns the arclength harmonic

\[
k_m = \frac{2\pi m}{L_N},
\qquad
\hat k_m = k_m D = \frac{2\pi mD}{L_N}.
\]

C006 supplies the dimensionless co-rigid temporal generator eigenvalue

\[
\hat\lambda_m = \hat\sigma_m + i\hat\omega_m .
\]

E012 follows the positive oscillatory member selected by C006 and records

\[
\hat\omega_m = \operatorname{Im}\hat\lambda_m > 0.
\]

For an independently supplied physical scale \(D_{\rm phys}\) and circulation
\(\Gamma\),

\[
\Omega_\Gamma = \frac{|\Gamma|}{4\pi D_{\rm phys}^2},
\quad
\omega_m = \Omega_\Gamma \hat\omega_m,
\quad
k_m = \frac{\hat k_m}{D_{\rm phys}}.
\]

A centered group velocity is

\[
v_{g,m}
\simeq
\frac{\omega_{m+1}-\omega_{m-1}}
{k_{m+1}-k_{m-1}},
\]

or equivalently

\[
v_{g,m}
=
\frac{|\Gamma|}{4\pi D_{\rm phys}}
\frac{\hat\omega_{m+1}-\hat\omega_{m-1}}
{\hat k_{m+1}-\hat k_{m-1}}.
\]

The SI conversion is disabled unless the physical-scale contract is explicitly
approved.
