# Scientific status — v0.4.0

- **Primary geometry population:** self-contained PTSA v1.0.0, 48 analytic trefoil candidates.
- **Seed promotion:** target-free dimensionless rolling/shape/mesh qualification; Universal-Action outcomes are not used to choose seeds.
- **Blind Universal Action discovery:** fully dimensionless; no canonical SST constant, SI scale, `h`, or `hbar` is permitted.
- **Mode definition:** one dedicated discovery probe per carrier/resolution; leading POD response is projected into the normal bundle and frozen before action amplitudes are evaluated.
- **Action definition:** energy and frequency are measured from the same frozen normal mode.
- **Frequency certification:** iterative dimensionless horizon extension; first-bin/sub-cycle estimates are unresolved, not physical frequencies.
- **Relative equilibrium:** the centerline gate uses the normal-projected residual; full marker residual remains diagnostic.
- **Mesh numerics:** adaptive arclength redistribution with pre-cleanup hard-gate diagnostics.
- **Coverage:** missing numerical cases cannot disappear silently; incomplete coverage is an explicit blind gate failure.
- **Absolute action comparison:** indeterminate unless a reveal-only independently sourced dimensional normalization is supplied and audited.
- **Legacy SST SI normalization:** contaminated negative control; cannot support an independent Planck prediction.
- **Dynamical backend:** regularized vortex-centerline Biot–Savart, not full three-dimensional finite-core Euler.
- **Topology:** PTSA construction is analytically trefoil-labelled, but this package does not elevate source labels into independent topology certification.
- **Floquet:** no accepted RPO means no true Floquet monodromy claim.
- **Production backend:** BASIC/EXTENDED/HIGHRES require native C++17/pybind11. Generation-time validation does not claim native parity when that extension is unavailable.
