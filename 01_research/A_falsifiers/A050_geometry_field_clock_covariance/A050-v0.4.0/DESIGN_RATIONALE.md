# Design rationale

The v0.3.2 result separates robust branch identity from nonstationary phase evolution. v0.4.0 therefore asks a mechanism question rather than repeating the existence test. Fresh perturbation holdouts reduce reuse of the original diagnostic data.

The recurrence test quotients the mode-dependent spatial action `C_m -> C_m exp(-i m theta)`, which is the appropriate modal representation of a common arclength/spatial phase shift. An RPO candidate is only a prerequisite for a later Newton/continuation + Floquet campaign.
