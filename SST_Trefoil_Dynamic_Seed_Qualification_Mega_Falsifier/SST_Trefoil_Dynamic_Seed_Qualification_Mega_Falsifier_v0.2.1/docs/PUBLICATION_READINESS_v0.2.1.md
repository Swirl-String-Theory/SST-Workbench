# Publication-readiness gate

Status: **NOT READY FOR A STABLE-RPO OR CAUSAL-MECHANISM CLAIM**.

v0.2.1 is a correctness release. Its projected Floquet calculation removes resolved translation, rotation, reparameterization and time-flow directions and now differentiates the same discretized orbital map used in S40. It remains a finite Fourier-normal projection rather than the full resolved filament tangent space.

Publication-oriented promotion requires:

1. a new held-out blind atlas with at least three independent source families;
2. N/dt/core/mesh-gauge convergence of the RPO residual and multiplier spectrum;
3. a full-state or validated Arnoldi/JVP monodromy calculation with explicit neutral-mode accounting;
4. uncertainty bounds that keep any stability conclusion on the permitted side of the frozen gate;
5. a preregistered intervention or ablation before using causal language;
6. claims restricted to the regularized filament/finite-core surrogate unless separate volumetric 3-D evidence is supplied.
