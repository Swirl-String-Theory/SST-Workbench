1 Identification and scope
A059 v0.2.0 is a self-contained, phased and scientifically nonblocking finite-model falsifier. It
vendors framework v1.0.6, embeds E011 v0.3.0 STATIC_READY provenance, and adds canonical
closed-braid dynamic probes. E011 remains a static source boundary; generated probes do not
upgrade it to independent dynamics-ready evidence.
2 Research question
Can the registered knot/link topologies support reproducible dynamic operator candidates for
family separation, signed central-core response, knot-specific tangent-map monodromy, circula-
tion conjugation and a three-component same-sign axial-core container?
3 Competing hypotheses
The null is that apparent dynamic structure is construction-dependent, numerically unstable,
universal rather than topology-specific, or insufficiently independent. The alternative requires
at least one registered dynamic operator to survive its numerical/symmetry controls; integrated
closure remains a separate stricter question.
4 Assumptions and boundary
All selected single-knot probes use the same closed-braid construction, smoothing and arclength
normalization. The three-component candidate has one straight+zcore vortex through each
ring centre, all three with equal positive circulation. The period lane uses only closed-loop
line-integral/residue mathematics. No physical interpretation is assigned to that mathematical
construction during blind analysis.
5 Symbols and units
All decision observables are dimensionless after the explicit v0.2.0 geometry/time normalization.
The finite-core radius and arclength are expressed in the normalized probe coordinate system.

8 Sources and provenance
The embedded E011 archive is SHA-256 pinned. Canonical probe braid words and generator
rules are frozen in the implementation commitment. They are mathematical representatives used
to test operators, not an independent replication of the upstream E011 geometry providers.
9 Gate semantics
G2 qualifies the source plus dynamic-probe foundation. G3 asks whether at least one dynamic
operator lane passes. G8 is deliberately hard and closes only if all registered required mechanism
sectors close independently. G9 verifies semantic reveal integrity.
10 Numerics and error controls
Finite-core Biot–Savart uses Python/NumPy FP64. P02 spans multiple curve resolutions and
finite-core radii. P03 spans multiple period-probe radii/resolutions. P04 requiresJ(−Γ)≈
−J(+Γ)and knot-to-knot monodromy separation larger than numerical fingerprint spread. P06
tests pairwise linking, three own-core periods and a recurrent dynamic cross-energy contrast
against an identity-braid unlink control.
11 Backend authority
Python/NumPy FP64 is authoritative in v0.2.0. No C++ or GPU lane is promoted.
12 Blindness and reveal
Blind outputs use HMAC anonymous object IDs. Semantic family roles, target pair/composite
hypotheses and conjugate-state interpretation remain private until commitment-verified reveal.
13 Statistics and multiplicity
The family test uses leave-one-out nearest-centroid classification on two frozen dynamic features
and exact enumeration of all 3-versus-4 label assignments. The period/response phase ranks the
complete registered pair search; the best pair is not treated as a population-significance claim.
