# Cross-workbench rationale for A047 v0.4.0

This design imports methodological lessons from other SST-Workbench investigations. These are planning inputs, not new evidence generated by A047-v0.4.0 itself.

## A038 — Trefoil Dynamic Seed Qualification

The relevant methodological lesson is ordering: source/geometry resolution, temporal convergence, core-radius robustness and mesh/gauge checks must precede expensive long dynamics or recurrence/Floquet interpretation. A later stage cannot rescue an upstream rejection. v0.4.0 therefore turns A047 into a staged funnel rather than simply rerunning the largest v0.3.0 amplifications for longer time.

## A046 — Relative-Vorticity Triadic Geometry

A046 showed that the local velocity gradient can be reconstructed consistently from strain plus relative-vorticity/antisymmetric content at floating-point scale in its tested flow fields. Because those fields were synthetic, A047 does not inherit any gravity interpretation. It only imports the kinematic identity as a mechanism-consistency diagnostic on actual PKLSA-driven Euler trajectories.

## A048 — Euler-vortex/core-resolution warning

The previous Euler-vortextube study produced numerically clean PDE diagnostics while reporting an under-resolved core (about 1.604 cells per sigma) and a large `3 sigma kappa_max` value (about 2.105). The lesson is that small divergence and energy drift do not prove that the initialized vortex core is physically/numerically resolved. This directly motivates S10 and the joint grid/slender-core feasibility estimate.

## A049 / Master-Factor Wave Closure

That line of work did not qualify a persistent transverse wave mode from pure Euler probes. A047 therefore does not reinterpret rapid vorticity amplification as an elastic/shear-wave or Master-Factor signal. Spectral/mechanism outputs may be handed off later, but the A047 verdict remains strictly Euler/BKM.

## Deferred to v0.5+

RPO/Floquet recurrence, finite-core SST regularization comparisons, and wave-closure interpretation remain separate downstream questions. They are intentionally not allowed to affect the v0.4.0 BKM verdict.
