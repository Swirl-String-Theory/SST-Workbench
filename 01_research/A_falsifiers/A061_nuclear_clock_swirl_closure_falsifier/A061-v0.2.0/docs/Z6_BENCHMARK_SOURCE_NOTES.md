# NiPS3 XY/Z6 references and adversarial controls

- Cheon, C.-Y. et al. (2025 preprint), *Nature of 2D XY antiferromagnetism in a van der Waals monolayer*, arXiv:2511.16065 (user-supplied 24-page PDF). Reported monolayer transitions ~124 K and ~60 K in magnetotransport. Interpreted with BKT and six-direction low-temperature order.
- Gao, F. Y. et al. (2026), *Six-state clock physics in an atomically thin antiferromagnet*, *Nature Materials*, DOI:10.1038/s41563-026-02516-7. User-supplied 41-page supplementary file; **not the supplement to Cheon et al.**
- Gao SI Note 2 eq. S3: `E_a=K6 cos(6 psi)` for an in-plane antiferromagnet, symmetry-derived in NiPS3; SI Note 3 eq. S4: `H=J sum cos(psi_r - psi_r') + K_q sum cos(q psi_r)`; competing `K2` anisotropy and finite-size tests in figs S19/S20.
- Objective: test an oscillator countermodel with q-fold phase pinning, not to import sixfold crystal symmetry as an SST particle property.
- Physical meaning: magnetic clock state counts angular preferred minima; nuclear optical clock measures Hz. Phase-only mode switches can alter measured frequency without clock-rate change.
- Evidence classes: NiPS3 experiments = external, independent *material benchmark*, never direct Th229/Swirl Clock measurement.
