# Mechanism contract

## CORE
A smooth two-scale cross-component interaction produces a repulsive inner core and weaker attractive shoulder. The field is a reduced finite-core stress surrogate only. It is not asserted to equal the Euler pressure-Poisson solution.

## ELASTIC
A discrete bending-energy gradient supplies a curvature-sensitive raw field. The tangent cross-product makes the injected velocity transverse to the raw variational force. This is a reduced transverse/curvature-elastic surrogate only. It is not evidence that an isotropic Euler fluid possesses a static shear modulus.

## Normalization
For each candidate and mechanism, the raw mechanism field is normalized once at the unperturbed initial geometry to the RMS baseline Biot–Savart velocity. That scale is then frozen for perturbations and trajectory evolution. The only scanned amplitude is the global dimensionless gain `g`, common to all candidates in an arm.
