# Changelog

## v0.1.0 — 2026-08-01

Initial research package.

### Added

- README containing the full scientific status and the ten principal audit findings;
- five separate follow-up research-track documents;
- self-contained NumPy dimensionless dynamical-ratio harness;
- Brian Gilbert ideal-knot AB parser;
- ring, trefoil, mirror-trefoil and figure-eight support;
- periodic arclength remeshing;
- sampled reach and curvature diagnostics;
- Rosenhead, Rankine and Winckelmans core kernels;
- best-fit relative translation/rotation modulo tangential gauge;
- relative-equilibrium residual;
- self-induction energy proxy;
- hydrodynamic impulse;
- RK4 evolution and recurrence diagnostics;
- quick and research campaign configurations;
- JSON and CSV outputs;
- tests, examples, source map and preregistration template.

### Epistemic guard

This release is a numerical research harness. It does not claim a finite-core Euler theorem, KAM stability, a particle identification, or an independent prediction of a dimensional constant.
