\section{Question and hypothesis}
Consider a 3D, incompressible and inviscid Euler carrier with global evolution parameter $T$. Its material vorticity $\boldsymbol\omega=\nabla\times\mathbf u$ must supply a reproducible intrinsic phase $\phi_K(T)$, a measured return time $\tau_K$, and a physical canonical momentum $p_K$ without imposing quantum action a priori. Distinguish local clock time $\tau_{\mathrm{clock},K}=\phi_K/\omega_{\mathrm{ref},K}$ from feedback delay $\tau_K$.
\section{Delay branch, stability and action}
The reduced phase-only hypothesis of SST-55 and SST-60 is
\begin{align}
\dot\phi(T)&=\Omega_0+K\sin[\phi(T-\tau_K)-\phi(T)],\\
\Omega&=\Omega_0-K\sin(\Omega\tau_K),\\
\lambda&=A(e^{-\lambda\tau_K}-1),\qquad A=K\cos(\Omega\tau_K).
\end{align}
For $A>0$, non-neutral characteristic exponents are stable. For $A\tau_K<-1$, there is an unstable real root; $\lambda=0$ expresses the neutral global-phase shift. In the strong-feedback regime stable branches approach $\Omega_n\tau_K\simeq 2\pi n$.
For a \emph{hypothesized} mechanical free rotor with fixed effective inertia $I$,
\begin{equation}
J_n=\oint p_\theta\,d\theta=2\pi I\Omega_n\simeq n\frac{4\pi^2I}{\tau_K}.
\end{equation}
For a \emph{distinct} harmonic generalized coordinate $q=a\cos\Omega T$ with constant mass $M$,
\begin{equation}
J_n=\int_0^{2\pi/\Omega}M\dot q^2\,dT=\pi M\Omega_n a_n^2\simeq n\frac{2\pi^2M a_n^2}{\tau_K}.
\end{equation}
Consequently a discrete frequency ladder does not imply a universal action spacing unless $I_n/\tau_{K,n}$ or $M_n a_n^2/\tau_{K,n}$ is physically restricted. Both action formulas require the actual vortex Hamiltonian reduction to justify their canonical status.
\section{Counterexample and physical barrier}
If an inviscid Euler solution $(\mathbf u,P)$ is available at fixed density, then $\mathbf u_\sigma(\mathbf x,T)=\sigma\mathbf u(\mathbf x,\sigma T)$ and $P_\sigma=\sigma^2P(\mathbf x,\sigma T)$ are also solutions. For a fixed-mass, same-shape kinetic action, $J_\sigma=\sigma J$. Hence standard ideal Euler dynamics alone do not fix a nonzero universal action quantum. The effective delay law additionally has attracting phase dynamics; its derivation as a reduced projection of the conservative parent flow is not yet established.
\section{Blinding and status}
Three input datasets are \emph{synthetic controls only}, separately hashed and anonymized. The physical gates requiring qualified 3D time-series, symplectic reduction, independent source replication and action-constant reveal are all \textsc{unresolved}. No quantum-mechanical equivalence is claimed.
