# A056 v0.1.0 preregistration

Freeze this document before inspecting scientific PKLSA campaign values.

## 1. Primary questions and nulls

**Q1 — phase closure.** Does a Sine–Gordon restoring term predict \(\varphi_{tt}\) better than the preregistered wave, linear Klein–Gordon and cubic Duffing alternatives?

Primary null: Sine–Gordon is not predictively superior after complexity penalty and held-out evaluation.

**Q2 — memory closure.** Does a Mittag-Leffler relaxation predict perturbation ringdown better than exponential, stretched-exponential and bi-exponential alternatives?

Primary null: Mittag-Leffler is not predictively superior to finite ordinary relaxation models.

## 2. Immutable gate order

`G0 provenance -> G1 admissibility -> G2 spectral qualification -> G3 phase competition -> G4 ringdown competition -> G5 independent replication -> G6 joint closure -> REVEAL`.

Every gate ends in `PASS`, `FAIL`, `UNRESOLVED`, or `NOT_RUN_PREREQUISITE`.

## 3. Independence unit

The independent unit is `source_group`, not mesh density, solver seed, timestep refinement, GPU replica, presentation variant or multiple perturbations of one carrier generated by the same provider configuration.

## 4. Blind boundary

Blind code and blind data must not contain topology names, particle labels, historical SST/VAM particle assignments, Standard-Model targets, or SST canonical numerical constants. The private reveal map is committed by SHA-256 before blind scoring.

The mathematical symbol \(\alpha\) is allowed only as the Mittag-Leffler fractional exponent and must not be used as a fine-structure target.

## 5. Phase-model statistic

For the generalized equation

\[
\varphi_{tt}=a\varphi_{ss}+F(\varphi),
\]

fit the four frozen candidate dictionaries W, KG, Duffing and SG by least squares. Evaluate predictive errors using four contiguous time-block folds. Let

\[
\mathrm{BIC}=n\ln(\mathrm{RSS}/n)+k\ln n.
\]

Define

\[
\Delta\mathrm{BIC}_{\rm SG}=\min_{j\ne SG}\mathrm{BIC}_j-\mathrm{BIC}_{SG}.
\]

SG support requires all of:

1. \(a>0\) and \(b>0\) in the full-data SG fit;
2. full-data SG design condition number \(\kappa_2\le10^8\);
3. \(\Delta\mathrm{BIC}_{SG}\ge10\);
4. \(\mathrm{NRMSE}_{SG}\le0.95\min_{j\ne SG}\mathrm{NRMSE}_j\).

No post-hoc harmonics, offsets or additional nonlinear terms may be added in v0.1.0.

## 6. Ringdown statistic

Normalize each accepted ringdown so \(R(0)=1\) with the provider-declared zero baseline. Split the series at 70% of the available time range. Parameters are estimated on the first 70%; the last 30% is held out.

ML support requires all of:

1. training \(\Delta\mathrm{BIC}_{ML}\ge10\) against exponential, stretched-exponential and bi-exponential competitors;
2. held-out \(\mathrm{NRMSE}_{ML}\le0.95\min\mathrm{NRMSE}_{competitor}\);
3. \(0.48<\hat\alpha<0.97\), preventing a boundary or exponential-limit fit from counting as fractional support.

## 7. Spectral qualification

POD is performed on the mean-centered phase matrix. Require numerical orthogonality residual below \(10^{-10}\). The top-3 split-window subspace overlap must be at least 0.80 for a PASS. If the phase matrix has rank below 3, use the available rank and record it.

## 8. Multiplicity and replication

Case-level support is reported for every opaque case. Family-level support requires reproduction in at least two independent source groups. Multiple cases from the same source group cannot independently satisfy G5.

## 9. CPU/GPU authority

CPU/Python is the reference. C++ CPU acceleration is allowed only after parity. GPU screening is not implemented in v0.1.0 and therefore cannot contribute scientific evidence.

## 10. Allowed conclusions

- `JOINT_SUPPORTED`: G3 and G4 are supported and G5 confirms both across at least two independent source groups.
- `PHASE_ONLY_SUPPORTED`: replicated G3 support without replicated G4 support.
- `MEMORY_ONLY_SUPPORTED`: replicated G4 support without replicated G3 support.
- `FALSIFIED_FOR_TESTED_REGIME`: qualified cases decisively prefer preregistered competitors for both claims.
- `UNRESOLVED`: insufficient admissible data, numerical qualification or independent replication.

Smoke controls can only produce `IMPLEMENTATION_VALIDATED` or `IMPLEMENTATION_FAILED`; never a scientific SST conclusion.
