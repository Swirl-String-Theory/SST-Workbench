\section{Identification, scope, and status}
A058 v0.1.4 is a recovery-analysis revision for the completed v0.1.3 KnotPlot pilot. It does not rerun KnotPlot. It imports the public v0.1.3 campaign acquisition read-only, corrects the framework gate-number collision, treats zero-byte KnotPlot CSV logs as missing, and derives the registered geometry diagnostics from the complete ASCII coordinate checkpoints.
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:QUESTION
\section{Research question}
What conclusions can be recovered rigorously from the completed v0.1.3 coordinate acquisition, and do plateau, resolution, generator, and historical-provenance requirements permit canonical KnotPlot export qualification?
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:HYPOTHESES
\section{Competing hypotheses}
$H_0$: the historical procedure is not canonical and one or more preregistered convergence requirements fail. $H_1$: recovered coordinate geometry satisfies the null/liveness controls and all required shape-convergence thresholds.
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:ASSUMPTIONS
\section{Assumptions, scope, and interpretive boundary}
v0.1.4 is same-data recovery, not independent replication. Zero-byte \code{metrics.csv} files are missing measurements and are never interpreted as zeros. Coordinate-derived statements may close a decision only where v0.1.3 already preregistered a coordinate-shape criterion. Relaxation remains a geometry-generation procedure and is not evidence of physical SST self-confinement.
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:SYMBOLS
\section{Symbols and dimensions}
$D_{\rm shape}$ is dimensionless normalized coordinate-shape discrepancy; $C_{\rm coord}$ is coordinate completeness; $f_{\rm plat}$ is the eligible-baseline plateau fraction; $\Delta_m$ is a relative scalar difference.
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:EQUATIONS
\section{Registered observables}
\begin{equation}
D_{\rm shape}=\min_{R,\delta,\sigma}\left[M^{-1}\sum_i\|\hat{x}_i-R\hat{y}_{\sigma i+\delta}\|^2\right]^{1/2}.
\end{equation}
\begin{equation}C_{\rm coord}=N_{\rm parseable}/N_{\rm expected}.\end{equation}
\begin{equation}\Delta_m=|m_c-m_0|/\max(|m_c|,|m_0|,10^{-12}).\end{equation}
\begin{equation}f_{\rm plat}=N(D_{10k,15k}\le0.005\wedge \Delta_{L/R_g}\le0.01)/N_{\rm eligible}.\end{equation}

% SST-REPORT-SECTION:GATES
\section{Gate DAG and decision semantics}
Framework-reserved lanes are not repurposed. G4 is independent confirmation, G5 CPU certification, G6 GPU screening, and G7 cross-source replication. Under the CPU recovery profile G4/G6/G7 are expected to be \code{NOT\_APPLICABLE}. A058-specific plateau/resolution/generator convergence is evaluated at G8, while G1 records the historical provenance decision. Diagnostic continuation remains allowed after G1 FAIL.
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:NUMERICS
\section{Numerics and thresholds}
The dstep null thresholds remain $10^{-10}$ for coordinate-derived scalar relative effect and $10^{-8}$ for $D_{\rm shape}$. Plateau requires $D_{\rm shape}\le0.005$ and relative $L/R_g$ drift $\le0.01$ for at least 80\% of eligible baselines. Resolution and anonymous same-topology generator comparisons require $D_{\rm shape}\le0.01$.
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:BACKENDS
\section{Backend authority and parity}
Python/NumPy FP64 is the reference analysis lane. C++17/pybind11 FP64 certifies coordinate-derived length, radius of gyration, and edge-CV with relative tolerance $10^{-12}$. No silent fallback may close G5.
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:BLINDNESS
\section{Blindness and reveal boundary}
Public analysis uses anonymous topology IDs. Construction identities remain under \code{private/}. The recovery import copies only public campaign artifacts; private v0.1.3 logs/scripts are neither needed nor imported. Reveal remains commitment-verified by Framework v1.0.6.
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:STATISTICS
\section{Statistics and multiplicity}
This is deterministic sensitivity/convergence analysis, not a population-significance claim. Same-software topology variants are controls rather than independent samples. A necessary preregistered shape-threshold failure is sufficient to reject canonical convergence even when an optional KnotPlot scalar log is missing.
