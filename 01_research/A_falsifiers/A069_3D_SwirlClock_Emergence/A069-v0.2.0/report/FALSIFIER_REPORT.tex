\begin{abstract}
The physical target is an independently predicted internal mode frequency from qualified
three-dimensional vortex dynamics, without a prescribed time dilation factor.
The v0.2.0 execution covers only two \emph{analytic numerical controls}: D1, an exact
unbounded affine three-dimensional Euler flow inherited from v0.1.0; and D2A,
a new steady smooth columnar vortex (two-dimensional motion embedded in three-dimensional
space). Neither tests a finite-length knotted Euler vortex or an atomic Swirl Clock.
The test instance is generated with framework v1.0.9 and conservatively prevents
physical PKLSA and relativity claims. The A069 registry slot remains provisional.
\end{abstract}

\section{Research question and scientific scope}
Can an independently sourced \texttt{DYNAMICS\_READY} finite-core 3D PKLSA vortex
support a gauge-invariant recurrent internal mode whose angular frequency is predicted
from initial hydrodynamic fields, not from a predetermined Lorentz factor or observed
clock frequency? A negative result on preregistered physical holdouts would reject
the proposed frequency closure \emph{within that qualified dynamical model}. D1 and D2A
provide implementation qualification only; they cannot settle this question.

$H_0$: no independently identifiable, numerically convergent recurrent physical

rate matches independent holdouts with signed, uncertainty-qualified agreement.
Both physical hypotheses remain \unresolved. The analytic reference cases are
not statistical samples of distinct physical vortex systems.

\section{Assumptions and boundary conditions}
The source medium is considered inviscid, barotropic and incompressible only
within each specified exact control. D1 has infinite energy in $\mathbb R^3$.
D2A has a smooth finite core and finite kinetic energy \emph{per unit axial length},
but is invariant along $z$, so its full three-dimensional energy is infinite.
Both controls have known trajectories by construction. No knot eigenmode or
physical time flow is inferred from a Lagrangian tracer's azimuth.
E010-v0.3.1/E011-v0.3.0 are admitted only as upstream geometry candidates,
not as dynamics or clock data. Legacy KnotPlot visually relaxed exports and
VortexLab proxy runs are not admitted as physical science sources.

\subsection{D1: affine vortex stretching and Kelvin circulation}
\begin{equation}
\mathbf u=A(T)\mathbf x,\quad
A=\begin{pmatrix}-s/2&-\Omega&0\\\Omega&-s/2&0\\0&0&s\end{pmatrix},\quad
\Omega(T)=\Omega_0 e^{sT},\quad R(T)=R_0e^{-sT/2}.
\end{equation}
Its exact 3D incompressible Euler pressure Hessian is
$\nabla\nabla p=-\rho\operatorname{sym}(\dot A+A^2)$. The Kelvin invariant is
$\Gamma=2\pi R^2\Omega=\mathrm{const}$; the independently integrated
Cartesian trajectory is compared with $\theta(T)=\Omega_0(e^{sT}-1)/s$,
using the continuous $s=0$ limit.
\subsection{D2A: smooth spatially inhomogeneous columnar Euler vortex}
\begin{align}
\mathbf u&=(-y\Omega(r),\ x\Omega(r),0),\quad
\Omega(r)=C e^{-r^2/a^2},\quad r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2},\\
\omega_z&=2C\left(1-\frac{r^2}{a^2}\right)e^{-r^2/a^2},\quad
\Gamma(r)=2\pi r^2\Omega(r),\\
\frac{dp}{dr}&=\rho r\Omega(r)^2,\quad
p(r)-p(0)=\frac{\rho C^2a^2}{4}\left(1-e^{-2r^2/a^2}\right),\\
\frac{E_{\rm kin}}{L_z}&=\frac{\pi\rho C^2a^4}{8},\qquad
\theta_{\rm pred}(T;r_0)=C e^{-(r_0/a)^2}T.
\end{align}
The final line gives the phase of \emph{one fluid tracer} at fixed $r_0$,
not the proper time of an atom. The Cartesian RK4 and midpoint solvers never
receive $\theta_{\rm pred}$ or a relativistic slowdown factor. They integrate
$\dot{\mathbf x}=\mathbf u(\mathbf x)$ and reconstruct angle with an unwrapped
$\mathrm{atan2}(y,x)$. The finite-difference Euler, curl and pressure gradients
are independently evaluated. A superposed rigid background rotation is a
reference-frame decomposition check, not an extra localized particle source.

\paragraph{Numerical thresholds and limits.}
The D2A control uses a fixed final time, two signed rates, three normalized
radii, a central Cartesian derivative step $10^{-5}a$, an exact pressure law,
and distinct RK4/midpoint integrators. Acceptance: phase error below $2\times10^{-7}$
rad; normalized radial error below $2\times10^{-7}$; momentum, divergence and
curl absolute residuals below $2\times 10^{-8}$; pressure relative error below
$3\times10^{-9}$. These are analytic-control precision tolerances and do not
represent uncertainties of a physical SST particle. The full three-dimensional
spatial resolution, core-model and pressure-Poisson convergence remain untested.
