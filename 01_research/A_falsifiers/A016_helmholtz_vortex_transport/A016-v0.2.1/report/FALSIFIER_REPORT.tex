% SST-REPORT-SECTION:QUESTION
\section{Research question and objective}
Can a smooth incompressible inviscid vortex model simultaneously satisfy: (i) material preservation of the zero/nonzero vorticity population; (ii) material-volume and vorticity-flux conservation; (iii) a locally irrotational but nontrivial exterior velocity field with circulation periods and nonlocal kinetic energy; and (iv) the original static relaxed-centerline relative-equilibrium test?

The objective is not to prove an SST interpretation. It is to make the Helmholtz-to-SST partition testable with explicit observables, provenance, convergence, backend certification, and geometry-provider robustness. The static centerline branch cannot establish actual long-time Euler persistence or reconnection avoidance. PKLSA is used as a geometry qualification/provenance layer and is not treated as an Euler-stability proof.

\section{Competing hypotheses and allowed conclusions}
\paragraph{Null hypothesis $H_0$.}
At least one preregistered consequence fails beyond tolerance, or the H2/H3/P3/P4/P5/P6 classifications are not reproducible across the required distinct upstream PKLSA provider groups for the same topology. Thus the material controls, exterior-field branch, unchanged centerline branch, and cross-source robustness branch are independently falsifiable.

\paragraph{Alternative hypothesis $H_1$.}
The exact material controls close numerically; the exterior is locally divergence-free/curl-free but can retain nonzero speed and circulation periods; a resolved energy fraction lies outside the near-core source zone; eligible closed sources have stable far-field scaling; each qualified PKLSA carrier receives an H0--H4/P3--P6 classification; and, wherever at least two distinct upstream provider groups exist for one topology, X0 satisfies its preregistered cross-source agreement criterion.

\paragraph{Falsification semantics.}
Each H* or P* branch reports independently after G0/G1/G2. Aggregate FAIL means at least one eligible input failed that branch; per-input results remain in \texttt{BLIND\_SAMPLE\_METRICS.json}. X0 is a separate provider-consistency result and does not overwrite the per-carrier classifications. Backend failure is certification evidence only. No threshold may be moved after freeze.

\section{Exact mathematical model and observables}
The central continuum relations are
\begin{align}
\vort &= \nabla\times\vel, \\
\vort(\mathbf{X}(\mathbf{a},t),t) &= \Fmat(\mathbf{a},t)\vort_0(\mathbf{a}), \\
J&=\det\Fmat=1, \\
\Phi_\omega&=\int_{S(t)}\vort\cdot d\mathbf{S}=\Gamma=\mathrm{const}. 
\end{align}
For the numerical filament reconstruction,
\begin{equation}
\vel(\mathbf{x})=\frac{\Gamma}{4\pi}\oint_C
\frac{d\boldsymbol{\ell}'\times(\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}')}{(|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'|^2+a^2)^{3/2}},
\end{equation}
with $a=0$ only for exterior evaluations that remain separated from the filament. The energy-length coefficient is
\begin{equation}
E=\rho\Gamma^2\ell_E,\qquad
\ell_E=\frac{1}{8\pi}\oint\!\oint
\frac{d\boldsymbol{\ell}\cdot d\boldsymbol{\ell}'}{\sqrt{|\mathbf{x}-\mathbf{x}'|^2+a^2}}.
\end{equation}
Relative equilibrium is tested after fitting rigid translation, rigid rotation, and tangential reparameterization. Exterior topology is tested through
\begin{equation}
\Gamma^{-1}\oint_\gamma\vel\cdot d\boldsymbol{\ell}\approx \operatorname{Lk}(\gamma,C).
\end{equation}
The exterior differential audit tests $\nabla\cdot\vel=0$ and $\nabla\times\vel=0$. The energy partition observable is
\begin{equation}
f_{\mathrm{ext}}=
\frac{\int_{V\setminus V_{\mathrm{core}}}\frac12|\vel|^2\,dV}
{\int_V\frac12|\vel|^2\,dV}.
\end{equation}
Far-field shells fit
\begin{equation}
\log u_{\mathrm{rms}}(r)=b-p\log r.
\end{equation}
A steady irrotational Bernoulli interpretation maps the frozen velocity exponent to $|\Delta p|\propto r^{-2p}$ and $|\nabla p|\propto r^{-(2p+1)}$; this is a post-fit interpretation, not a fitting constraint.
For X0, a provider-collapsed classification agreement is
\begin{equation}
A_{T,g}=\frac{\max_s n_s(T,g)}{N_{\mathrm{provider}}(T,g)},
\end{equation}
where $s$ is one of PASS, FAIL, INELIGIBLE, or INTERNAL\_INCONSISTENCY. The frozen acceptance threshold is $A_{T,g}\ge0.80$ with at least two distinct upstream provider groups.

This is primarily a deterministic numerical falsifier, not a parameter-fitting study. The per-carrier sampling unit is one PKLSA-qualified carrier. For X0, the vote unit is one upstream provider group within one topology; multiple variants from the same provider are not independent. H*/P* aggregate status is conservative: any eligible per-input failure makes the aggregate branch FAIL. P6 eligibility is fixed by vector-area ratio before exponent acceptance. \section{Interpretation, limits, falsifiable predictions, and minimal follow-up experiments}
P0--P2 test the exact material-population logic of the smooth incompressible model, not a full time-integrated knot evolution. H3 remains the original strong static-centerline shape test and can fail while the matter--field partition branch remains informative. P3/P4 establish whether the computed exterior is locally potential yet globally circulation-bearing. P5 tests whether velocity-energy support extends beyond the near-core source zone. P6 supplies a frozen far-field exponent for later mechanism comparison. X0 separately determines whether the H2/H3/P3/P4/P5/P6 classifications are robust across distinct upstream PKLSA provider groups for the same topology; it is a representation-dependence test, not an assertion that topology uniquely fixes dynamics.

A minimal next experiment after v0.2.1 is a genuinely time-dependent Euler/finite-core evolution in which material tracers are initialized both inside and outside a vortex tube, then tested for support-class preservation, circulation conservation, and topology change under simultaneous spatial and temporal refinement. A second independent experiment should reconstruct pressure from the time-dependent velocity field rather than infer pressure solely from a steady exterior relation.
