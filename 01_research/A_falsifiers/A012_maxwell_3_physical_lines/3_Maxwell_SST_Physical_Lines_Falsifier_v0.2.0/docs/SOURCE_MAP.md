# Source map

The workbench is motivated by James Clerk Maxwell's *On Physical Lines of Force* (1861--1862):

- Part I: local stress described by principal pressures/tensions and an axisymmetric vortex interpretation;
- dynamic-pressure scaling proportional to density and velocity squared;
- force density obtained from spatial variation/divergence of internal stress;
- later parts: electric displacement and field-energy/momentum analogies.

Only the mathematical/mechanical structures are used here. Maxwell's literal molecular-vortex/idle-wheel microphysics is not assumed.

The centreline input format and Ridgerunner metadata are specific to the SST KnotPlot pipeline and are not claims from Maxwell.
