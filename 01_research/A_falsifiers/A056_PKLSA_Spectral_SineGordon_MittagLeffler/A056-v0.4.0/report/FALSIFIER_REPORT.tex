% SST-REPORT-SECTION:QUESTION
\section{Question}
For source-native E010/PKLSA-qualified vortex carriers evolved by a preregistered finite-core filament or incompressible Euler provider, does a phase-topology-aware spectral qualifier identify a stable low-dimensional Kelvin residual field, and if so do Sine--Gordon restoring dynamics and/or Mittag--Leffler fractional-memory relaxation survive blind discovery, held-out confirmation and numerical certification?

% SST-REPORT-SECTION:HYPOTHESES
\section{Hypotheses}
The phase null family consists of Wave, Klein--Gordon and Duffing laws; Sine--Gordon is the phase candidate. The relaxation null family consists of exponential, stretched-exponential and bi-exponential laws; Mittag--Leffler is the fractional-memory candidate. Passing G2 is only an admissibility result. A physical conclusion additionally requires canonical-framework eligible independent/experimental replication at G7.

% SST-REPORT-SECTION:ASSUMPTIONS
\section{Assumptions and interpretation boundary}
The provider freezes the paired Kelvin residual phase before scoring. The phase is circular, $\varphi\equiv\varphi+2\pi$, so the v0.4.0 blocking representation uses $z=e^{i\varphi}$. The v0.3.0 $m=3$ filament run is development/motivation evidence only and cannot close any v0.4.0 gate. Official v0.4.0 E010 campaigns use the preregistered fresh perturbation $m=4$, $\epsilon=0.03$. Python/NumPy FP64 is the reference lane, C++/OpenMP FP64 is certification, and SYCL FP32/DD32 remains screening-only. In conservative inviscid dynamics, projected ringdown is interpreted as transfer/dephasing unless an independent dissipative mechanism is supplied.

\subsection{Circular/Fourier G2 representation}
The circular embedding and per-time spatial centering are
\begin{equation}
z(s,t)=e^{i\varphi(s,t)},\qquad \widetilde z(s,t)=z(s,t)-\langle z(\cdot,t)\rangle_s.
\end{equation}
If $\sigma_j$ are singular values of the time-by-space matrix $\widetilde z$, the top-$k$ circular-POD energy fraction is
\begin{equation}
\eta_k^{\rm circ}=\frac{\sum_{j=1}^{k}\sigma_j^2}{\sum_j\sigma_j^2}.
\end{equation}
For each time sample,
\begin{equation}
Z_n(t)=\frac{1}{N_s}\sum_{j=0}^{N_s-1}e^{i\varphi(s_j,t)}e^{-2\pi i n j/N_s}.
\end{equation}
For a time window $W$, excluding the spatial mean mode $n=0$,
\begin{equation}
P_n^W=\frac{\langle |Z_n(t)|^2\rangle_{t\in W}}
{\sum_{m\ne0}\langle |Z_m(t)|^2\rangle_{t\in W}},\qquad n\ne0,
\end{equation}
and the discovery/confirmation spectral overlap is the Bhattacharyya coefficient
\begin{equation}
\mathcal O_F=\sum_{n\ne0}\sqrt{P_n^{D}P_n^{C}},\qquad 0\le\mathcal O_F\le1.
\end{equation}
The blocking G2 thresholds are preregistered as
\begin{equation}
\eta_3^{\rm circ}\ge0.95,\qquad
\epsilon_{\rm orth}^{\rm circ}\le10^{-10},\qquad
\mathcal O_F\ge0.90.
\end{equation}
The historical raw-$\varphi$ early/late POD overlap and the circular-POD early/late subspace overlap are diagnostics only; neither can block or rescue G2 in v0.4.0.

\subsection{Real-provider construction}
The frozen Kelvin perturbation and observables are
\begin{align}
\mathbf X_\epsilon(s,0)&=\mathbf X_0(s)+\epsilon[\cos(m\theta)\mathbf e_1+\sin(m\theta)\mathbf e_2],\\
\dot{\mathbf X}(s,t)&=\mathbf u(\mathbf X(s,t),t),\\
q(s,t)&=\delta\mathbf X\cdot\mathbf e_1+i\,\delta\mathbf X\cdot\mathbf e_2,\\
\varphi(s,t)&=\arg[q(s,t)e^{-im\theta(s)}],\\
R(t)&=\frac{|c_m(t)|}{|c_m(0)|},\qquad c_m=N_s^{-1}\sum_jq(s_j,t)e^{-im\theta_j}.
\end{align}
The volumetric lane evolves the 3-D incompressible unforced Euler equations with a 2/3-dealiased pseudo-spectral RK4 method and a Gaussian tangent-tube finite-core seed. The cheaper filament lane evolves a regularized Biot--Savart centerline directly. Both lanes are simulation evidence and cannot by themselves close physical G7.

\section{Numerics and convergence}
Phase support still requires positive Sine--Gordon coefficients, condition number $\le10^8$, discovery $\Delta\mathrm{BIC}\ge10$, and the preregistered held-out NRMSE ratio and absolute ceiling. Memory support uses the same discovery/holdout logic plus $0.48<\alpha<0.97$. Provider and score configuration SHA-256 values are embedded in the frozen science contract.

For $\mathbf c=(a,b)$,
\[
\delta_c=\frac{\|\mathbf c_f-\mathbf c_c\|_2}{\max(\|\mathbf c_f\|_2,10^{-30})}.
\]
For memory,
\[
\delta_\alpha=|\alpha_f-\alpha_c|,\qquad
\delta_\tau=\frac{|\tau_f-\tau_c|}{\max(|\tau_f|,10^{-30})}.
\]
Production profiles additionally require A056 C++ FP64 parity $\le10^{-10}$. The ringdown observable and all downstream model-selection thresholds are unchanged from v0.3.0; only the G2 spectral admission test is scientifically replaced in v0.4.0.

\section{Interpretation}
A pass of circular/Fourier G2 means only that the residual phase has a stable preregistered low-dimensional/cross-window spectral representation under the selected invariances.
