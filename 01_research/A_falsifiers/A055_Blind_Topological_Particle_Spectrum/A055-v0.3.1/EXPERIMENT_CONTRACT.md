# Experiment contract

A055 v0.3.1 is a method-corrected reanalysis, not an independent physical replication. It consumes two already completed sealed simulation campaigns:

1. authoritative three-component finite-core compound dynamics;
2. historical isolated-centerline controls.

The compound projected Jacobian is recomputed at the sealed finest resolution using the exact isolated upstream blind runner. The recomputed eigenvalue multiset must reproduce the stored sealed spectrum before any new directional statistic is accepted.

A mode's propagation sign is inferred from the spatial Fourier direction of its reconstructed complex eigenfield under a positive-temporal-frequency convention. Complex conjugation therefore does not create a second direction automatically.

The transverse projection uses a periodic Bishop parallel-transport frame with distributed closed-loop holonomy correction, and a traveling candidate must have at least 50% of projected coefficient power in the registered Kelvin mode family.
