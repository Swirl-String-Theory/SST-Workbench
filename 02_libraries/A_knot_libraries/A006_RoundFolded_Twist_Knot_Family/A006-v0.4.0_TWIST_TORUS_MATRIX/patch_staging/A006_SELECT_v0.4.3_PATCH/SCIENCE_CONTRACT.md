# Science contract

1. Geometry: full-component near-unknot link construction is a mathematical candidate; neither topological linked-sum isotopy nor Milnor mu123 has been independently certified.
2. Mirroring is coordinate parity (one-axis reflection), preserving Euclidean lengths and absolute curvatures. A knot being amphichiral is an ambient-isotopy property and is **not** established by equality of dynamic or geometric scalar proxies. Orientation-sensitive measures may change sign under reflection.
3. The Fremlin symmetry table is prior/reference classification only, not a discovery from this campaign. It lists 4_1 as amphichiral and 3_1 as chiral; many higher cases need independent lookup.
4. KnotPlot bead mechanics (mech/elec forces, optional bugged tanf) are **not** incompressible inviscid Euler evolution, nor independent proof of SST torus rolling.
5. Resampling to 256 and 512 vertices may change polygonal knot topology in areas of close passage. Export is labelled STATIC_GEOMETRY_CANDIDATE. Recheck actual imported topology and every checkpoint before interpreting results.
6. The N-based comparison is meaningful only at controlled bead spacing, contact threshold, force law and solver configuration. Fixed `close=0.20` and bead-dependent Hookean interactions may introduce severe numerical dependence, as shown in prior v0.4.2d output audit.
7. All data and outcomes remain separately classified as PLAN, PREPARED_NOT_EXECUTED, RUNTIME_CHECKPOINTS_COMPLETE or FAIL_CLOSED. There are no invented runtime measurements.
