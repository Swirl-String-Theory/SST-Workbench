% SST-REPORT-SECTION:IDENTIFICATION
\section{Identification, scope, and status}
A058 v0.1.3 is a blind parameter-sensitivity and relaxation-robustness study of the historical KnotPlot export pipeline. It is pinned to SST Falsifier Framework v1.0.6. The v0.1.3 maintenance revision adds the framework-generated 256-bit private key/nonce material required by FREEZE, adds fail-closed private-material preflight, and makes this report conform to the v1.0.6 section-marker contract. Scientific thresholds and KnotPlot parameter ranges are unchanged from v0.1.2.
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:QUESTION
\section{Research question}
Are historical KnotPlot-relaxed exports robust to relaxation, force, discretization and export settings, and do the historical scripts qualify as a canonical production geometry source?
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:HYPOTHESES
\section{Competing hypotheses}
$H_0$: the exported geometry is materially dependent on one or more undocumented/default relaxation, numerical, generator, resolution, or output-format choices, so the historical export protocol is not qualified as canonical. $H_1$: after explicit output-format control, the preregistered null control, numerical envelope, relaxation plateau, resolution ladder, and same-topology generator comparison remain within the registered tolerances.
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:ASSUMPTIONS
\section{Assumptions, scope, and interpretive boundary}
Relaxation is treated as a geometry-generation algorithm. A relaxed embedding is not evidence of intrinsic SST self-confinement or particle stability. KnotPlot trajectories are simulation evidence, not independent experimental replication. Multi-component links are evaluated with component-safe scalar observables where a single closed-curve Procrustes comparison is not applicable.
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:SYMBOLS
\section{Symbols and dimensions}
All primary sensitivity statistics below are dimensionless after normalization. $m$ denotes a scalar KnotPlot observable, $D_{\rm shape}$ a normalized coordinate-shape discrepancy, $C_V(\ell)$ edge-length coefficient of variation, $P_{\rm prov}$ historical provenance completeness, and $C_{\rm camp}$ campaign completeness.
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:EQUATIONS
\section{Registered observables}
For scalar metric $m$,
\begin{equation}\Delta_m=\frac{|m_c-m_0|}{\max(|m_0|,10^{-12})}.\end{equation}
For coordinate curves, a scale-free cyclic/reversal-aware Procrustes diagnostic is
\begin{equation}
D_{\rm shape}=\min_{R,\delta,\sigma}\left[\frac1M\sum_i\|\hat x_i-R\hat y_{\sigma i+\delta}\|^2\right]^{1/2}.
\end{equation}
Edge discretization is monitored through
\begin{equation}C_V(\ell)=\sigma(\ell_i)/\bar\ell.\end{equation}

% SST-REPORT-SECTION:ALGORITHM
\section{Exact algorithm and campaign}
The preregistered pilot contains one same-topology dual-generator pair, one symmetry control, two additional single-component cases, and one multi-component link. It tests the full on/off cube of mechanical/electrical/bending forces, \code{max-dr}, \code{close}, \code{bencon}, \code{stusplit}, a bead-resolution ladder, and \code{dstep} as a null control. Checkpoints are 0, 250, 1000, 4000, 10000 and 15000 atomic relaxation iterations. Every generated coordinate export explicitly requests ASCII representation.
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:SOURCES
\section{Source, provenance, and independence contract}
The supplied historical archive contains 50 scripts: 20 knot, 16 link and 14 torus constructions. Their principal relaxation settings are constant across the archive and therefore do not constitute a parameter-sensitivity experiment. Their \code{save} commands do not explicitly pin \code{ascii/raw}; output representation therefore depends on KnotPlot's runtime \code{sformat} state. The new campaign records the KnotPlot executable identity, runtime \code{version}, full \code{parameters} state, generated script hashes, and explicit ASCII coordinate outputs. Generator-family comparisons are controls within the same software family, not independent physical replication.

All scientific gates G1--G8 depend only on G0, not on one another. Therefore an early FAIL never prevents later diagnostic gates from running. G8 may reject the historical protocol on provenance while G3--G7 still determine replacement settings.

% SST-REPORT-SECTION:NUMERICS
\section{Numerical certification and preregistered thresholds}
The \code{dstep} null control must remain below $10^{-10}$ in scalar relative effect. Numerical-envelope median scalar effect must remain at or below $10^{-2}$. Baseline 10k--15k drift must remain at or below $10^{-2}$. Coordinate thresholds are $D_{\rm shape}\le0.005$ for plateau/parameter robustness and $D_{\rm shape}\le0.01$ for the resolution comparison.
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:BACKENDS
\section{Backend authority and parity}
Python/NumPy FP64 is the reference analysis lane. The instance-local C++17/pybind11 FP64 kernel is a certification lane for the registered geometry metrics. Native FP64 relative parity tolerance is $10^{-12}$. No silent backend substitution may close G5.
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:BLINDNESS
\section{Blindness and reveal boundary}
Public computation uses anonymous topology identifiers. Construction identities and semantic notes remain under \code{private/}. Framework v1.0.6 creates nonced commitments for the private forbidden-term list and reveal mapping at FREEZE and later verifies the frozen protocol and reveal integrity before disclosure. The private nonce and opaque-ID key are 256-bit instance material generated once for this version and are never regenerated during a run.
% SST-REPORT-SECTION:STATISTICS
\section{Statistics and multiplicity}
A058 is primarily a deterministic sensitivity, convergence, and invariance study rather than a population-significance test. Effect summaries are evaluated against preregistered tolerances; force ablations are diagnostic perturbations and the \code{dstep} sweep is a negative control. Multiple topologies test robustness across geometry classes but do not constitute independent physical samples.

% SST-REPORT-SECTION:INTERPRETATION
\section{Allowed interpretation}
A G8 FAIL means the historical export protocol should not be promoted as canonical. It does not imply that its geometries are unusable. G3--G7 determine whether old geometries can remain comparison/legacy candidates and which parameter envelope should be frozen for a replacement canonical generation. PASS cannot be interpreted as intrinsic vortex stability or as a physical particle-stability result.
