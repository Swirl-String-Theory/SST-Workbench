# High-resolution synthesis of the seven-article conversation

## Executive result

The seven articles do **not** supply seven new SST laws.  They supply a coherent certification architecture.  Across acoustics, nonlinear optics, magnetic textures, quantum critical spectroscopy, acoustic analogy transport, and cosmography, the same methodological pattern recurs:

\[
\boxed{
\text{geometry} \rightarrow \text{physical field} \rightarrow \text{constraint/closure}
\rightarrow \text{probe operator} \rightarrow \text{phase/spectrum} \rightarrow \text{observable}
}
\]

None of these arrows may be inferred merely because the endpoints look similar.  This is the strongest canon-level lesson of the seven-paper review.

## Article-by-article transfer map

### 1. Observation of conformal field theory spectra in a quantum simulator

**What the source establishes.**  Universal low-energy structure can be certified experimentally by finite-size scaling, energy-ratio convergence, symmetry/parity sector selection, boundary-condition control, and—when individual levels become dense—a dynamical structure factor rather than forced peak assignment.

**SST transfer.**  A proposed Kelvin/Floquet/internal clock spectrum must be certified across numerical resolution, core regularization, perturbation sector and boundary/environment class.  A single visually dominant FFT peak is insufficient.  If individual modes merge, use a correlation spectrum/dynamical structure factor or resolvent-like observable.

**Do not import.**  Ising or tricritical-Ising CFT ratios are not SST predictions.

### 2. Acoustic toroidal vortices with programmable links and knots

**What the source establishes.**  A scalar acoustic phase field can carry torus-linked or knotted equiphase fibers while the underlying singularity ring and material medium have different topology.  Integer winding, component connectivity, mutual linking and self-knotting are separate descriptors.  Geometric closure does not guarantee propagation invariance.

**SST transfer.**  Freeze four distinct labels:

\[
\mathcal K_{\rm center},\qquad
\mathcal K_{\omega},\qquad
\mathcal K_{\Phi},\qquad
\mathcal K_{\rm null}.
\]

A phase-fiber trefoil does not prove a vorticity-tube trefoil.  A central hole in a centerline or amplitude plot does not prove a threaded material-vorticity channel.

### 3. A source-term interpretation of turbulent rough wall-pressure spectra

**What the source motivates and orthodox incompressible mechanics supplies.**  Pressure must be treated as a nonlocal Poisson-constrained field.  For constant-density incompressible Euler flow,

\[
\boxed{
\nabla^2\!\left(\frac{p}{\rho_{\!f}}\right)
=\frac12|\boldsymbol\omega|^2-S_{ij}S_{ij}
}
\]

with

\[
S_{ij}=\frac12(\partial_j u_i+\partial_i u_j).
\]

For a free-space decaying solution,

\[
\boxed{
\frac{p(\mathbf x)-p_\infty}{\rho_{\!f}}
=-\frac{1}{4\pi}\int
\frac{\frac12|\boldsymbol\omega(\mathbf x')|^2-S:S(\mathbf x')}
{|\mathbf x-\mathbf x'|}\,d^3x'
}
\]

with the Green function changed for bounded/periodic domains.

**SST transfer.**  This should become the general pressure closure guard.  The local Bernoulli defect remains a restricted stationary/streamline relation, not a generic three-dimensional knot pressure law.

### 4. Topologically Configurable Nonlinear Vortex Generation at van der Waals Heterostructures

**What the source establishes.**  A phase singularity and quantized orbital winding can be generated by coherent interference of local nonlinear sources with controlled relative phase.  A central dark core can therefore be a wave-interference singularity rather than a material vortex.  Finite angular segmentation produces modal impurity/aliasing.

**SST transfer.**  For any phase-feedback-delay clock, separate

\[
\Delta\Phi_{\rm loop}
=\Delta\Phi_{\rm dyn}+\Delta\Phi_{\rm geom}
+\Delta\Phi_{\rm coupling}+\cdots
\]

and require

\[
\Delta\Phi_{\rm loop}=2\pi n
\]

under sampling refinement.  Vary the number of phase stations to distinguish physical modes from sampling aliases.

### 5. Wormhole Geometry from a Magnetic Vortex

**What the source establishes.**  A microscopic texture can induce an **effective probe metric** while independent Berry/holonomy and scalar-potential channels coexist.  The ideal metric is core-sensitive near the vortex.  Geometry-like response can be even under winding reversal while phase/holonomy channels encode different signed/parity information.

**SST transfer.**  Do not posit a metric first.  Linearize the chosen probe sector and derive its principal symbol.  Only then may one write schematically

\[
\mathcal P(x,k)=g_{\rm eff}^{\mu\nu}(x)k_\mu k_\nu.
\]

Separate circulation-reversal channels:

\[
O_{\rm even}=\frac{O(+\Gamma)+O(-\Gamma)}{2},\qquad
O_{\rm odd}=\frac{O(+\Gamma)-O(-\Gamma)}{2}.
\]

Expose finite-core ratios instead of fitting them away.

### 6. A transport geometry of acoustic analogies

**What the source establishes.**  Exact formulations can assign the same physics differently between “source” and “propagation”.  On shell, physical observables agree.  Off shell, approximate transported models can exhibit representation holonomy.  Finite-step holonomy that disappears under refinement is not continuum curvature; noncommuting continuum deformations can yield a genuine commutator curvature.

**SST transfer.**  If two SST formulations are declared equivalent, demand observable invariance.  A phase/frequency shift under a closed chain of re-descriptions is not physical until it survives step refinement and cannot be explained by Euler/incompressibility residuals or omitted transformation terms.  Test order reversal \(AB\) versus \(BA\) and, where applicable, the \(\epsilon^2\) commutator scaling.

### 7. Late-Time Cosmic Acceleration in Hořava–Lifshitz Gravity

**What the source demonstrates methodologically.**  A flexible kinematic reconstruction can fit observations without being uniquely derived from the claimed gravity dynamics.  Derived quantities and extrapolations must be audited for domain regularity and algebraic/numerical consistency.

**SST transfer.**  Separate a theory-independent kinematic reference from an independently derived SST cosmological prediction.  Before interpretation, scan for poles/branch domains, high-redshift asymptotics, transition-redshift reproduction, age-integral reproduction, and complexity-penalized comparison.

## Cross-paper canon architecture

### Layer 0 — epistemic status

Every statement is tagged separately as orthodox identity, SST-derived result, calibration, ansatz, bridge/theorem target, or research diagnostic.  Compound block labels are insufficient when one paragraph mixes these categories.

### Layer 1 — geometry

Inputs: centerline coordinates, ropelength/reach, component number, Gauss centerline linking, curvature/contact diagnostics.

No phase or volume-field claims are promoted here.

### Layer 2 — physical Euler field

Inputs: \(\mathbf u(\mathbf x,t)\), \(p(\mathbf x,t)\), \(\boldsymbol\omega\), core regularization, boundary conditions.

Required residuals include

\[
R_{\rm div}=\nabla\cdot\mathbf u
\]

and

\[
R_P=\nabla^2(p/\rho_{\!f})-
\left(\frac12\omega^2-S:S\right).
\]

### Layer 3 — phase/clock

A phase observable must be physically defined before winding is calculated.  Quantization of a fitted or visualization phase is not enough.  Phase source terms and sampling aliases are logged separately.

### Layer 4 — probe/effective geometry

A metric-like response is derived from a probe equation.  It is initially an effective propagation geometry, not a claim that the Euclidean substrate itself has become curved.

### Layer 5 — dynamical/spectral certification

Require resolution/core/domain convergence, perturbation-sector stability, repeatability, Floquet/monodromy consistency where available, and correlation-spectrum checks where modes become dense.

### Layer 6 — representation invariance

Equivalent formulations must agree on observables on shell.  Closed-loop defects are subjected to step refinement and commutator tests.

### Layer 7 — cosmological/inference guard

Curve-fitting does not identify dynamics.  Every phenomenological fit requires an independent theory-to-fit parameter map and model comparison against standard references.

## Blind-falsifier philosophy

The blind package deliberately returns `INDETERMINATE` rather than manufacturing fields from geometry.  A relaxed KnotPlot/Ridgerunner curve can test geometry and resolution, but it cannot by itself test pressure-Poisson closure, phase winding, effective metric, or dynamical spectroscopy.  Those gates activate only when the corresponding sidecars are supplied by an independent solver/measurement pipeline.

The intended promotion chain is therefore

\[
\boxed{
\text{centerline certification}
\rightarrow
\text{resolved Euler field}
\rightarrow
\text{pressure closure}
\rightarrow
\text{physical phase/probe definition}
\rightarrow
\text{dynamic/spectral stability}
\rightarrow
\text{representation invariance}
}
\]

and not the reverse.
