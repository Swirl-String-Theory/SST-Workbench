\section{Seven-Source Closure, Phase, Spectral, and Holonomy Certification}
\label{sec:rt_seven_source_certification}

\paragraph{Status.}
\textbf{[RESEARCH-TRACK / BLIND FALSIFICATION PROGRAMME].}
The source systems reviewed here provide experimental or theoretical
precedents for diagnostic methods.  Their system-specific numerical laws are
not SST predictions.

\subsection{Spectral universality and boundary-sector gate}
\textbf{[ORTHODOX SPECTROSCOPY PRECEDENT / SST RESEARCH TEST].}
A proposed carrier mode \(\omega_j\) must be tested over spatial resolution,
core regularization, perturbation class, and boundary/environment class.
For a family indexed by resolution \(N\), certification requires a converged
limit
\begin{align}
\omega_j(N)\longrightarrow\omega_j^{(\infty)}
\end{align}
and stable mode ratios only when such ratios were preregistered from an
independent SST derivation.  No Ising- or tricritical-Ising CFT ratio is
imported as an SST target.  When individual peaks become dense, the preferred
observable is a dynamical correlation spectrum/structure factor rather than a
forced multi-peak assignment.

\subsection{Phase-fiber and centerline separation}
\textbf{[RESEARCH TOPOLOGY GUARD].}
For a resolved internal phase \(\Phi\) on a closed carrier define
\begin{align}
W_\Phi
=\frac{1}{2\pi}\oint d\Phi.
\end{align}
A phase winding is accepted as a physical internal mode only if
\(W_\Phi\) converges to the same integer under sampling refinement and the
phase observable is independently defined by the SST dynamics.  The knot
class of an equiphase fiber is recorded separately from
\(\mathcal K_{\rm center}\) and \(\mathcal K_\omega\).

\subsection{Phase-source ledger and sampling aliases}
\textbf{[RESEARCH METHODOLOGY].}
When a phase rate admits a physical decomposition, record
\begin{align}
\Delta\Phi_{\rm loop}
=\Delta\Phi_{\rm dyn}
+\Delta\Phi_{\rm geom}
+\Delta\Phi_{\rm coupling}
+\cdots .
\end{align}
Closure requires \(\Delta\Phi_{\rm loop}=2\pi n\), but the integer alone does
not identify which term generated it.  A discrete \(N\)-station analysis must
also vary \(N\); aliases that move with the sampling lattice are numerical,
whereas a physical azimuthal mode remains at fixed mode number.

\subsection{Pressure-Poisson source ledger}
\textbf{[ORTHODOX IDENTITY / REQUIRED SST CLOSURE GATE].}
For every resolved volume-field run archive separately
\begin{align}
Q_\omega&=\frac12|\boldsymbol\omega|^2,\\
Q_S&=S_{ij}S_{ij},\\
Q_P&=Q_\omega-Q_S.
\end{align}
The differential residual is
\begin{align}
R_P
=\nabla^2(p/\rho_{\!f})-Q_P,
\end{align}
and the independently reconstructed Green solution must converge to the
solver pressure under grid and domain refinement.  Core, near-field, far-field,
and boundary contributions are to be reported separately when a central
pressure channel is claimed.

\subsection{Circulation reversal and effective-probe geometry}
\textbf{[RESEARCH CLASSIFIER / NO AUTOMATIC ATTRIBUTION].}
For an observable \(O\) measured in otherwise identical \(+\Gamma\) and
\(-\Gamma\) runs define
\begin{align}
O_{\rm even}&=\frac{O(+\Gamma)+O(-\Gamma)}{2},\\
O_{\rm odd}&=\frac{O(+\Gamma)-O(-\Gamma)}{2}.
\end{align}
Pressure-Poisson sources built quadratically from velocity gradients are even
under complete velocity reversal.  Odd response is therefore a separate
channel and must not be merged with the even pressure/geometry channel
without an explicit coupling theorem.  If a one-scale effective geometry is
proposed, its observable must additionally pass a finite-core collapse test
with the second dimensionless ratio \(a_{\rm core}/a_{\rm eff}\) exposed rather
than fitted away.

\subsection{Representation holonomy and continuum-refinement gate}
\textbf{[RESEARCH METHODOLOGY / CRUCIAL FALSIFIER].}
For a closed sequence of equivalent descriptions let \(H_N\) denote the
return map when the same smooth path is discretized into \(N\) steps.  Define
\begin{align}
\epsilon_N=\|H_N-I\|.
\end{align}
A finite-step representation artifact satisfies
\begin{align}
\epsilon_N\rightarrow0
\qquad (N\rightarrow\infty).
\end{align}
A claimed continuum holonomy requires a nonzero converged residue and an
independently predicted local commutator/curvature coefficient.  Order reversal
\(AB\) versus \(BA\) and on-shell residuals
\begin{align}
R_{\rm div}&=\nabla\cdot\mathbf u,\\
\mathbf R_E&=\partial_t\mathbf u+(\mathbf u\cdot\nabla)\mathbf u
+\rho_{\!f}^{-1}\nabla p
\end{align}
must be included in the ledger.

\subsection{Cosmological reconstruction guard}
\textbf{[RESEARCH INFERENCE PROTOCOL].}
Any future SST expansion history must be produced in two tracks: an
unconstrained kinematic reference fit and an independently derived SST
dynamical prediction.  Before interpretation, automatically test for poles,
branch points, complex domains, high-redshift matter recovery, transition
redshift reproduction, age-integral reproduction, and model-selection
penalties.  A kinematic fit is not a derivation of the SST gravity sector.
