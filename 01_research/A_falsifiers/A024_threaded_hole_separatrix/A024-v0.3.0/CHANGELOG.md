## v0.2.0

Methodological release driven by the first v0.1.1 extended results.

- **Independent-unit correction:** inferential sign tests now aggregate repeated settings within each carrier first. A knot scanned at four beta values contributes one carrier-level vote, not four independent samples.
- **Strict geometry qualification:** exact finite segment-segment clearance must exceed `min_initial_gap_core` (default `2.5 a`) before a carrier/thread geometry may enter the blind campaign. The current Fremlin `TWIST_6_1` configuration is excluded by this gate at the standard core/thread settings rather than being allowed to stop at tau=0.
- **Hierarchical contact gate:** a trajectory that contacts before the full horizon receives `FAIL_CONTACT`; truncated AUC/RPO/Floquet values never enter ordinary pair scoring. Full-horizon beats truncated; if both truncate, only survival time is compared.
- **Centralized thread closure:** far-return legs of multi-thread bundles leave in separate radial sectors, removing artificial thread-thread proximity generated by the v0.1 shared return direction.
- **Pressure-law falsifier:** new pressure-only campaign scans beta from -2 to +2 and post-seal fits `Delta p = A beta + B beta^2 + C beta^3 + D beta^4`, plus symmetric even/odd estimates.
- **Free exponent:** radial pressure is fit blind to `p(r)=A+B/r^nu`; `nu=1` is only inspected after reveal. Legacy 1/r and 1/r^2 fits remain diagnostic.
- **Far-field convergence:** dedicated pressure-only ladder varies both grid resolution and box size. A gravity closure cannot pass v0.2 from a single finite periodic pressure solve.
- **Stability islands:** dedicated long discovery scan over beta, thread count and helix turns. Best scanned settings are explicitly labelled discovery-only and require a fresh confirmation campaign.
- **Triple gear:** geometric cyclic phase rates for the three unknot components and threaded helix are measured; the best small rational winding is discovered after dynamics. No mechanical ratio is supplied as a target.
- **Density mode:** `thread_coupling_mode=per_thread_beta` is available to distinguish fixed-per-thread circulation from fixed-total bundle circulation.
- Windows UTF-8 and MSVC/`py::ssize_t` fixes from v0.1.1 are retained.
