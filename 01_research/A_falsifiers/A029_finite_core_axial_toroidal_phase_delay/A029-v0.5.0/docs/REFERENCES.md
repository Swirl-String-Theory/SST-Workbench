# References / comparison basis

- Howard, L. N. & Gupta, A. S. (1962), *On the hydrodynamic and hydromagnetic stability of swirling flows*, **Journal of Fluid Mechanics 14**(3), 463–476. DOI: `10.1017/S0022112062001366`. The package uses an equivalent primitive-variable linearized incompressible-Euler formulation rather than inserting an empirical delay law.
- Leibovich, S., Brown, S. N. & Patel, Y. (1986), *Bending waves on inviscid columnar vortices*, **Journal of Fluid Mechanics 173**, 595–624. DOI: `10.1017/S0022112086001283`. Their finite-core columnar-vortex analysis motivates treating dispersion branches and group velocity as physical observables.
- Hattori, Y. & Fukumoto, Y. (2014), *Modal stability analysis of a helical vortex tube with axial flow*, **Journal of Fluid Mechanics 738**, 222–249. DOI: `10.1017/jfm.2013.591`. Their curved finite-core analysis motivates the axial-flow/curvature/torsion extension that should follow if this slender local-to-global gate survives.

These references motivate the tested ingredients; no literature stability target, delay, or preferred return phase is supplied to blind scoring.
