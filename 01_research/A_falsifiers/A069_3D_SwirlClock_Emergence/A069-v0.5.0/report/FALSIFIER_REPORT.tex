\subsection{Finite-volume, fully 3D nonlinear Schrodinger model}
We integrate the normalized Hamiltonian GP equation,
\begin{equation}
 i\partial_T\Psi=-\frac12\nabla^2\Psi+(|\Psi|^2-1)\Psi,
 \qquad \mathbf{x}\in[-L_b/2,L_b/2)^3.
\end{equation}
The modulus of the initialized field vanishes in a finite-width tube about the closed seed curve $K$. The \emph{phase} is constructed instead from the oriented solid angle $\Omega_{\Sigma}(\mathbf{x})$ of a spanning 2-chain $\Sigma$ whose boundary is $K$:
\begin{equation}
 \Psi_{\rm raw}(\mathbf{x},0)=\tanh\!\left(\frac{d(\mathbf{x},K)}{\sqrt2\xi}\right)
 \exp\left[\frac{i\Omega_{\Sigma}(\mathbf{x})}{2}\right].
\end{equation}
Because $\Omega_{\Sigma}$ changes by $4\pi$ across a spanning sheet, $\exp(i\Omega_{\Sigma}/2)$ remains single-valued and has local $2\pi$ vortex circulation. A smooth real window $\chi(\mathbf{x})$ supported outside the vortex region interpolates the field to the uniform condensate at the periodic faces:
\begin{equation}
 \Psi_0=1+\chi(\Psi_{\rm raw}-1).
\end{equation}
The taper is \emph{preparation-dependent}: the resulting $\Psi_0$ is not assumed to be a relaxed stationary GP knot. We require exact periodic-face agreement and nonzero plaquette phase-winding counts.

Split-step spectral Strang integration advances the full $N_g^3$ complex array in absolute dimensionless time $T$; mass $M$, GP energy $E$, and vortical plaquettes are monitored:
\begin{equation}
 M=\int|\Psi|^2d^3x,\qquad
 E=\int\left(\frac12|\nabla\Psi|^2+\frac12(|\Psi|^2-1)^2\right)d^3x.
\end{equation}
Plaquette counts are coarse, mesh-dependent indicators, \emph{not} a continuous topological linking invariant. A count change alone does not demonstrate a specific reconnection event.
