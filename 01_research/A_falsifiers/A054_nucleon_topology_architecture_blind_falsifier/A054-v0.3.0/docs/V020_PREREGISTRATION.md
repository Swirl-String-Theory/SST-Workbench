# v0.2.0 preregistration

The v0.1.1 finding is frozen as motivation, not as a pass/fail target:

- global linked architecture dominated the short-horizon v0.1.1 observables;
- the Triple-Gear/T(3,3) family showed a small but provider-persistent tendency for the 6_1 component to occupy the one-opposed circulation slot;
- Borromean candidates showed strong 2+1 polarity selection but no comparable 5_2/6_1 slot identity preference.

v0.2.0 attempts to break those observations.

### BASIC cohort
All six mixed 5_2/6_1 assignments are tested in both linked skeletons for same-provider E011 strata. Canonical 001/011 unlinked cells are included as negative controls.

### EXTENDED cohort
Same geometry classes, but all Cartesian E011 provider pairs are admitted.

### FULL cohort
All three architectures × all six mixed assignments × all provider pairs.

Every geometry is evaluated under Q0=(+++), Q1=(-++), Q2=(+-+), Q3=(++-). No slot is designated "preferred" inside the blind runner.

### Decision discipline
There is no weighted winner score. Reveal reports blocked contrasts: certification fraction, local growth, ringdown boundedness, RPO incidence and conditional Floquet multipliers. A provider-sensitive result remains provider-conditional.

### Additional integrity locks
- Relative-equilibrium residual is diagnostic only; no hard cutoff is fitted from the v0.1.1 33/33/33 clusters.
- Nonlinear evolution removes component-wise tangential velocity as a marker-reparameterization gauge.
- Floquet is null/not-evaluated without an accepted nontrivial RPO.
- The energy Hessian remains diagnostic because positive curvature is not sufficient for Hamiltonian vortex stability.
